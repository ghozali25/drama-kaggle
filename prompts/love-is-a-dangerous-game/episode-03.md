# Episode 03 — Pasangan Ditukar (6 Scene × 5 detik)

Format: Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B, image-to-video, 480p, 24 fps.
Total: ~30 detik. Cocok untuk Shorts / Reels / TikTok vertikal (720x1280 di render,
atau render landscape lalu crop vertikal di CapCut).

## Kunci karakter (COPY-PASTE PERSIS di semua scene)

> **OLIVIA, wanita 28 tahun, rambut coklat panjang, gaun merah marun, kalung emas tipis.**
> **RYAN, pria 32 tahun, suami Olivia, jas navy, rambut hitam rapi, jam tangan mahal.**
> **JAKE, pria 33 tahun, suami Mia, kemeja putih lengan digulung, rambut coklat gelap, tatapan tenang, jam kulit hitam.**
> **MIA, wanita 29 tahun, sahabat Olivia, istri Jake, rambut pirang pendek, gaun hitam.**

Foto referensi: buat 1 foto Olivia + 1 foto Ryan + 1 foto Jake + 1 foto Mia dulu
(Dreamina / Gemini image), upload foto itu sebagai gambar awal di tiap scene
(image-to-video). Jangan ganti deskripsi di tengah episode.

## Setting global

- Negative prompt (tempel di semua scene):
  `blurry, low quality, distorted face, extra fingers, deformed hands, watermark, text, logo`
- Kamera: slow push-in / close-up, jangan pakai gerakan kamera ekstrem di T4 480p.
- Dialog: Wan 2.2 5B tidak bisa diandalkan untuk dialog Indonesia yang jelas.
  Render **tanpa suara dulu**, dubbing belakangan (CapCut TTS / ElevenLabs free),
  lalu tempel subtitle bakar di edit.

## Scene 1 — Hook (0–5 detik)

**Visual:** Malam hari, ruang utama vila. Semua pasangan berkumpul. Tuan rumah memegang mangkuk undian berisi kartu nama.

**Prompt:**
> A mysterious villa host in a grey suit holding a bowl of name cards in a grand living room at night,
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace, Ryan,
> 32-year-old man, Olivia's husband, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch, Jake,
> 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze,
> black leather watch, and Mia, 29-year-old woman, Olivia's best friend, Jake's wife,
> short blonde hair, black dress, gathered around, warm light, camera wide shot slowly pushing in, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Malam ini, pasangan akan diundi ulang."

## Scene 2 — Undian (5–10 detik)

**Visual:** Ryan mengambil kartu dari mangkuk. Wajahnya berubah tegang saat membaca nama di kartu itu.

**Prompt:**
> Ryan, 32-year-old man, Olivia's husband, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch,
> drawing a name card from a bowl, his face turning tense as he reads the card,
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace,
> watching him anxiously, camera close-up on the card, slow push-in

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Ini tidak mungkin..."

## Scene 3 — Pengumuman (10–15 detik)

**Visual:** Tuan rumah mengumumkan hasil undian: Olivia dipasangkan dengan Jake, Ryan dengan Mia. Olivia terkejut.

**Prompt:**
> A mysterious villa host in a grey suit announcing the draw results in a grand living room,
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace, looking shocked,
> Jake, 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled sleeves, dark brown hair,
> calm gaze, black leather watch, standing calmly beside her, camera medium shot static, tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Olivia dengan Jake. Ryan dengan Mia. Berlaku sampai retreat selesai."

## Scene 4 — Tatapan Jake (15–20 detik)

**Visual:** Jake menatap Olivia dengan tenang, tidak kaget sama sekali. Olivia balas menatap, bingung.

**Prompt:**
> Jake, 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled sleeves, dark brown hair,
> calm gaze, black leather watch, looking at Olivia calmly, not surprised at all, Olivia,
> 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace, staring back confused,
> warm lamp light, camera close-up, slow push-in, suspenseful

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Kamu... tidak kaget?"

## Scene 5 — Protes Ryan (20–25 detik)

**Visual:** Ryan berdiri dan memprotes, tapi Mia menahan lengannya sambil tersenyum dipaksakan.

**Prompt:**
> Ryan, 32-year-old man, Olivia's husband, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch,
> standing up to protest, Mia, 29-year-old woman, Olivia's best friend, Jake's wife,
> short blonde hair, black dress, holding his arm with a forced smile,
> other guests watching silently, camera low angle, tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Ryan, duduk. Ini cuma permainan."

## Scene 6 — Ending / Cliffhanger (25–30 detik)

**Visual:** Close-up kartu undian di tangan Ryan — nama di kartu itu sudah ditulis tangan, bukan dicetak. Layar menghitam. Teks: "Bersambung..."

**Prompt:**
> Close-up of a name card in Ryan's hand, 32-year-old man, Olivia's husband, navy suit,
> neat black hair, expensive watch, the name on the card handwritten, not printed,
> dim light, slowly fading to black, dramatic, cinematic, sharp detail

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Episode 4 — undian ini sudah diatur sejak awal?"
