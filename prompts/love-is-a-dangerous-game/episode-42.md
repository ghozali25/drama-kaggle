# Episode 42 — Di Tepi Danau (6 Scene × 5 detik)

Format: Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B, image-to-video, 480p, 24 fps.
Total: ~30 detik. Cocok untuk Shorts / Reels / TikTok vertikal (720x1280 di render,
atau render landscape lalu crop vertikal di CapCut).

## Kunci karakter (COPY-PASTE PERSIS di semua scene)

> **OLIVIA, wanita 28 tahun, rambut coklat panjang, gaun merah marun, kalung emas tipis.**
> **RYAN, pria 32 tahun, suami Olivia, jas navy, rambut hitam rapi, jam tangan mahal.**
> **JAKE, pria 33 tahun, suami Mia, kemeja putih lengan digulung, rambut coklat gelap, tatapan tenang, jam kulit hitam.**
> **MIA, wanita 29 tahun, sahabat Olivia, istri Jake, rambut pirang pendek, gaun hitam.**

Foto referensi: buat 1 foto tiap karakter dulu (Dreamina / Gemini image),
upload foto itu sebagai gambar awal di tiap scene (image-to-video).
Jangan ganti deskripsi di tengah episode.

## Setting global

- Negative prompt (tempel di semua scene):
  `blurry, low quality, distorted face, extra fingers, deformed hands, watermark, text, logo`
- Kamera: slow push-in / close-up, jangan pakai gerakan kamera ekstrem di T4 480p.
- Dialog: Wan 2.2 5B tidak bisa diandalkan untuk dialog Indonesia yang jelas.
  Render **tanpa suara dulu**, dubbing belakangan (CapCut TTS / ElevenLabs free),
  lalu tempel subtitle bakar di edit.

## Scene 1 — Fajar di Tepi Danau (0–5 detik)

**Visual:** Fajar menyingsing, danau tenang, asap tipis masih mengepul dari vila di kejauhan. Olivia duduk di tepi danau.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, woman 28 years old, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> sitting at the edge of a calm lake at dawn, thin smoke rising from a villa in the distance,
> looking at the water, quiet and thoughtful, soft morning light, camera wide shot slowly moving closer, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Semalam, semuanya berubah. Dan aku tidak bisa kembali seperti dulu."

## Scene 2 — Ryan Mendekat (5–10 detik)

**Visual:** Ryan berjalan mendekat dari belakang, berhenti beberapa langkah dari Olivia, menatapnya dengan ragu.

**Prompt:**
> Ryan, man 32 years old, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch,
> walking toward Olivia, woman 28 years old, long brown hair, maroon red dress,
> at the edge of a lake at dawn, stopping a few steps behind her, hesitant,
> soft morning light, camera medium shot, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Olivia, boleh aku duduk sebentar?"

## Scene 3 — Ryan Meminta Maaf (10–15 detik)

**Visual:** Ryan duduk di samping Olivia, menatap danau. Wajahnya lelah tapi jujur.

**Prompt:**
> Ryan, man 32 years old, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch,
> sitting beside Olivia, woman 28 years old, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> at the edge of a lake at dawn, both looking at the water,
> Ryan speaking honestly, tired but sincere face, camera close-up, soft morning light

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Maafkan aku. Aku membawamu ke retreat itu karena takut kehilanganmu — justru karena itu aku kehilanganmu."

## Scene 4 — Olivia Menjawab (15–20 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia menoleh ke Ryan, tersenyum tipis dan sedih, lalu menatap danau lagi.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, woman 28 years old, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> turning to Ryan, man 32 years old, navy suit, neat black hair,
> at the edge of a lake at dawn, giving a faint sad smile, then looking back at the water,
> calm and resolved face, camera close-up, soft morning light

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Aku memaafkanmu, Ryan. Tapi memaafkan bukan berarti kembali."

## Scene 5 — Ryan Melepaskan Olivia (20–25 detik)

**Visual:** Ryan melepas jam tangannya, lalu berdiri dan mengulurkan tangan untuk berjabat tangan dengan Olivia — sebagai perpisahan yang bermartabat.

**Prompt:**
> Ryan, man 32 years old, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch,
> standing up at the edge of a lake at dawn and offering a handshake to Olivia,
> woman 28 years old, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> Olivia standing and shaking his hand, both calm, soft morning light, camera medium shot, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Kalau begitu, aku melepaskanmu. Pilihlah jalanmu sendiri, Olivia."

## Scene 6 — Ending / Cliffhanger (25–30 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia berdiri sendiri di tepi danau, menatap matahari terbit, lalu layar menghitam. Teks: "Bersambung..."

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, woman 28 years old, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> standing alone at the edge of a lake at sunrise, looking at the rising sun,
> calm and free, soft golden light, camera slow push-in,
> slowly fading to black, dramatic, cinematic, sharp detail

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Episode 43 — finale. Awal yang baru di luar vila."
