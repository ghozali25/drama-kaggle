# Episode 26 — Jebakan Makan Malam (6 Scene × 5 detik)

Format: Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B, image-to-video, 480p, 24 fps.
Total: ~30 detik. Cocok untuk Shorts / Reels / TikTok vertikal (720x1280 di render,
atau render landscape lalu crop vertikal di CapCut).

## Kunci karakter (COPY-PASTE PERSIS di semua scene)

> **OLIVIA, wanita 28 tahun, rambut coklat panjang, gaun merah marun, kalung emas tipis.**
> **RYAN, pria 32 tahun, suami Olivia, jas navy, rambut hitam rapi, jam tangan mahal.**
> **JAKE, pria 33 tahun, suami Mia, kemeja putih lengan digulung, rambut coklat gelap, tatapan tenang, jam kulit hitam.**
> **MIA, wanita 29 tahun, sahabat Olivia, istri Jake, rambut pirang pendek, gaun hitam.**

Foto referensi: buat 1 foto Olivia + 1 foto Ryan + 1 foto Jake + 1 foto Mia dulu (Dreamina / Gemini image),
upload foto itu sebagai gambar awal di tiap scene (image-to-video).
Jangan ganti deskripsi di tengah episode.

## Setting global

- Negative prompt (tempel di semua scene):
  `blurry, low quality, distorted face, extra fingers, deformed hands, watermark, text, logo`
- Kamera: slow push-in / close-up, jangan pakai gerakan kamera ekstrem di T4 480p.
- Dialog: Wan 2.2 5B tidak bisa diandalkan untuk dialog Indonesia yang jelas.
  Render **tanpa suara dulu**, dubbing belakangan (CapCut TTS / ElevenLabs free),
  lalu tempel subtitle bakar di edit.

## Scene 1 — Hook (0–5 detik)

**Visual:** Malam hari di ruang makan vila. Meja panjang dengan lilin menyala, empat gelas anggur tersusun rapi. Pemilik vila tersenyum di ujung meja.

**Prompt:**
> A long dining table with candles and four wine glasses in a lakeside villa dining room at night,
> an old villa owner smiling at the far end of the table, Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress,
> entering the room cautiously, camera slow push-in, cinematic, tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Selamat datang di makan malam terakhir kita di vila ini."

## Scene 2 — Undangan yang Tak Bisa Ditolak (5–10 detik)

**Visual:** Ryan, Jake, dan Mia duduk di kursi masing-masing. Olivia ragu-ragu sebelum duduk, menatap gelas-gelas di meja.

**Prompt:**
> Ryan, 32-year-old man, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch, Jake, 33-year-old man, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze,
> and Mia, 29-year-old woman, short blonde hair, black dress, sitting at a candlelit dining table,
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, hesitating before sitting, camera medium shot, tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Duduklah, Olivia. Malam ini kita akhiri semua permainan."

## Scene 3 — Jake Curiga (10–15 detik)

**Visual:** Jake menatap gelas-gelas itu dengan tajam. Ia memperhatikan pelayan yang menuang anggur dengan tangan gemetar.

**Prompt:**
> Jake, 33-year-old man, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch,
> staring sharply at the wine glasses on a candlelit dining table, noticing a waiter pouring wine with trembling hands,
> night, camera close-up, slow movement, tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Ada yang tidak beres dengan gelas-gelas ini."

## Scene 4 — Racun (15–20 detik)

**Visual:** Close-up salah satu gelas: cairan di dalamnya sedikit lebih keruh dari gelas lain. Tangan Jake bergerak pelan di bawah meja.

**Prompt:**
> Close-up of one wine glass on a candlelit dining table, its liquid slightly cloudier than the others,
> Jake's hand, 33-year-old man, black leather watch, moving slowly under the table,
> night, camera close-up, dramatic, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Satu gelas di antaranya beracun. Aku bisa melihatnya."

## Scene 5 — Pemilik Vila Mengangkat Gelas (20–25 detik)

**Visual:** Pemilik vila mengangkat gelasnya, mengajak semua bersulang. Ryan ikut mengangkat gelasnya tanpa curiga.

**Prompt:**
> An old villa owner raising his wine glass for a toast at a candlelit dining table at night,
> Ryan, 32-year-old man, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch, raising his glass without suspicion,
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, watching in fear, camera slow push-in, tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Untuk kejujuran. Siapa pun yang berbohong malam ini, akan membayar."

## Scene 6 — Ending / Cliffhanger (25–30 detik)

**Visual:** Close-up mata Olivia yang ketakutan menatap gelas Ryan, lalu layar menghitam. Teks: "Bersambung..."

**Prompt:**
> Close-up of Olivia's frightened eyes, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> staring at Ryan's wine glass, dark background, slowly fading to black,
> dramatic, cinematic, sharp eye detail

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Ryan, jangan minum gelas itu!"
