# Episode 08 — Foto Lama (6 Scene × 5 detik)

Format: Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B, image-to-video, 480p, 24 fps.
Total: ~30 detik. Cocok untuk Shorts / Reels / TikTok vertikal (720x1280 di render,
atau render landscape lalu crop vertikal di CapCut).

## Kunci karakter (COPY-PASTE PERSIS di semua scene)

> **OLIVIA, wanita 28 tahun, rambut coklat panjang, gaun merah marun, kalung emas tipis.**
> **RYAN, pria 32 tahun, suami Olivia, jas navy, rambut hitam rapi, jam tangan mahal.**
> **JAKE, pria 33 tahun, suami Mia, kemeja putih lengan digulung, rambut coklat gelap, tatapan tenang, jam kulit hitam.**
> **MIA, wanita 29 tahun, sahabat Olivia, istri Jake, rambut pirang pendek, gaun hitam.**

Foto referensi: buat 1 foto Olivia + 1 foto Ryan + 1 foto Jake + 1 foto Mia dulu
(Dreamina / Gemini image), upload foto itu sebagai gambar awal di tiap scene
(image-to-video). Jangan ganti deskripsi di tengah episode.

## Setting global

- Negative prompt (tempel di semua scene):
  `blurry, low quality, distorted face, extra fingers, deformed hands, watermark, text, logo`
- Kamera: slow push-in / close-up, jangan pakai gerakan kamera ekstrem di T4 480p.
- Dialog: Wan 2.2 5B tidak bisa diandalkan untuk dialog Indonesia yang jelas.
  Render **tanpa suara dulu**, dubbing belakangan (CapCut TTS / ElevenLabs free),
  lalu tempel subtitle bakar di edit.

## Scene 1 — Hook (0–5 detik)

**Visual:** Sore hari, dermaga danau. Jake duduk sendirian, memegang sebuah foto lama yang sudah menguning.

**Prompt:**
> Jake, 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled sleeves, dark brown hair,
> calm gaze, black leather watch, sitting alone on a lake pier in the afternoon,
> holding an old yellowed photograph, calm lake behind, camera medium shot slowly pushing in, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Foto ini... sudah lama sekali."

## Scene 2 — Olivia Mendekat (5–10 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia berjalan ke dermaga dan duduk di samping Jake. Jake ragu-ragu, lalu menunjukkan foto itu padanya.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace,
> walking onto a lake pier in the afternoon and sitting beside Jake, 33-year-old man,
> Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze,
> black leather watch, Jake hesitating, then showing her the old photograph,
> calm lake behind, camera medium shot static

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Boleh aku lihat?"

## Scene 3 — Wajah yang Dikenal (10–15 detik)

**Visual:** Foto lama itu menampilkan vila yang sama, bertahun-tahun lalu. Di antara orang-orang di foto, ada wajah yang dikenal Olivia.

**Prompt:**
> Close-up of an old yellowed photograph showing the same lakeside villa years ago,
> a group of people standing in front of it, Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair,
> maroon dress, thin gold necklace, holding the photo, her eyes widening at a familiar face,
> lake pier in the afternoon, camera close-up on the photograph, slow push-in

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Wajah ini... aku pernah melihatnya. Di mana?"

## Scene 4 — Jake Menjelaskan (15–20 detik)

**Visual:** Jake berkata foto itu diambil di vila ini, jauh sebelum retreat. Ia menyimpan foto itu karena seseorang di dalamnya menghilang.

**Prompt:**
> Jake, 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled sleeves, dark brown hair,
> calm gaze, black leather watch, explaining quietly on a lake pier in the afternoon,
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace,
> listening intently, calm lake behind, camera close-up, suspenseful

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Foto ini diambil di vila ini. Seseorang di dalamnya menghilang, dan tidak pernah ditemukan."

## Scene 5 — Ryan Mengawasi (20–25 detik)

**Visual:** Dari jendela vila, Ryan mengawasi Olivia dan Jake di dermaga. Wajahnya dingin, tangannya mengepal.

**Prompt:**
> Ryan, 32-year-old man, Olivia's husband, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch,
> watching Olivia and Jake on the lake pier from a villa window, face cold, fist clenched,
> afternoon light, camera medium shot, slow push-in, tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Mereka terlalu dekat. Ini harus berhenti."

## Scene 6 — Ending / Cliffhanger (25–30 detik)

**Visual:** Close-up foto lama itu — wajah yang dikenal Olivia ternyata mirip dengan tuan rumah vila, hanya lebih muda. Layar menghitam. Teks: "Bersambung..."

**Prompt:**
> Close-up of an old yellowed photograph, a young face in the group resembling the villa host,
> only younger, Olivia's hand holding the photo, lake light, slowly fading to black,
> dramatic, cinematic, sharp detail

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Episode 9 — tuan rumah itu sudah ada di vila ini sejak dulu?"
