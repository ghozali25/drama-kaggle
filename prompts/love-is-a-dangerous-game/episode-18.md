# Episode 18 — Rekaman CCTV (6 Scene × 5 detik)

Format: Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B, image-to-video, 480p, 24 fps.
Total: ~30 detik. Cocok untuk Shorts / Reels / TikTok vertikal (720x1280 di render,
atau render landscape lalu crop vertikal di CapCut).

## Kunci karakter (COPY-PASTE PERSIS di semua scene)

> **OLIVIA, wanita 28 tahun, rambut coklat panjang, gaun merah marun, kalung emas tipis.**
> **RYAN, pria 32 tahun, suami Olivia, jas navy, rambut hitam rapi, jam tangan mahal.**
> **JAKE, pria 33 tahun, suami Mia, kemeja putih lengan digulung, rambut coklat gelap, tatapan tenang, jam kulit hitam.**
> **MIA, wanita 29 tahun, sahabat Olivia, istri Jake, rambut pirang pendek, gaun hitam.**

Foto referensi: buat 1 foto Olivia + 1 foto Ryan + 1 foto Jake + 1 foto Mia dulu
(Dreamina / Gemini image), upload foto itu sebagai gambar awal di tiap scene
(image-to-video). Jangan ganti deskripsi di tengah episode.

## Setting global

- Negative prompt (tempel di semua scene):
  `blurry, low quality, distorted face, extra fingers, deformed hands, watermark, text, logo`
- Kamera: slow push-in / close-up, jangan pakai gerakan kamera ekstrem di T4 480p.
- Dialog: Wan 2.2 5B tidak bisa diandalkan untuk dialog Indonesia yang jelas.
  Render **tanpa suara dulu**, dubbing belakangan (CapCut TTS / ElevenLabs free),
  lalu tempel subtitle bakar di edit.
- Lokasi: ruang keamanan vila, layar CCTV, malam hari setelah dansa.

## Scene 1 — Hook (0–5 detik)

**Visual:** Jake menarik Olivia ke ruang keamanan vila yang sepi, menunjuk layar CCTV yang menyala.

**Prompt:**
> Jake, a 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch,
> pulling Olivia, a 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace, into a quiet villa security room at night, pointing at glowing CCTV screens,
> tense, close-up, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Lihat ini sebelum mereka menghapusnya."

## Scene 2 — Rekaman Diputar (5–10 detik)

**Visual:** Layar CCTV menampilkan Ryan bertemu seorang pria berjas hitam di taman vila, diam-diam, tengah malam.

**Prompt:**
> A CCTV screen showing Ryan, a 32-year-old man, Olivia's husband, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch,
> meeting a man in a black suit secretly in a villa garden at midnight,
> watched by Olivia, a 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace, and Jake, a 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch, in a security room at night,
> grainy CCTV image, close-up on screen, tense, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Itu Ryan. Tengah malam, di taman — bertemu pemilik vila."

## Scene 3 — Wajah Olivia (10–15 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia menutup mulut, matanya berkaca. Rekaman itu tidak bisa dibantah.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, a 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> covering her mouth, eyes wet, watching a CCTV screen in a villa security room at night,
> Jake, a 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch, beside her,
> close-up, emotional, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Ryan bilang dia tidak pernah bertemu pemilik vila. Rekaman ini berkata lain."

## Scene 4 — Jake Menjelaskan (15–20 detik)

**Visual:** Jake menunjuk jam di pojok rekaman — pertemuan itu terjadi malam sebelum mereka tiba di vila.

**Prompt:**
> Jake, a 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch,
> pointing at the timestamp on a CCTV screen in a villa security room at night,
> Olivia, a 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace, listening intently,
> close-up, tense, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Lihat jamnya. Pertemuan ini terjadi semalam sebelum kita tiba. Ryan sudah merencanakan semuanya."

## Scene 5 — Suara Langkah (20–25 detik)

**Visual:** Jake menyalin rekaman ke ponselnya, lalu keduanya membeku — langkah kaki mendekat ke ruang keamanan.

**Prompt:**
> Jake, a 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch,
> copying CCTV footage to his phone in a villa security room at night, freezing as footsteps approach,
> Olivia, a 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace, holding her breath beside him,
> tense, close-up, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Cepat. Seseorang datang."

## Scene 6 — Ending / Cliffhanger (25–30 detik)

**Visual:** Layar CCTV tiba-tiba mati satu per satu, ruangan gelap. Lalu layar menghitam. Teks: "Bersambung..."

**Prompt:**
> CCTV screens turning off one by one in a dark villa security room at night,
> Olivia, a 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace, and Jake, a 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch, in darkness,
> then slowly fading to black, dramatic, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Episode 19 — siapa sebenarnya pemilik vila ini?"

---

Cliffhanger: Rekaman CCTV membuktikan Ryan bertemu pemilik vila diam-diam — lalu semua layar mati.
