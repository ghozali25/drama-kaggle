# Episode 35 — Ryan Berbalik (6 Scene × 5 detik)

Format: Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B, image-to-video, 480p, 24 fps.
Total: ~30 detik. Cocok untuk Shorts / Reels / TikTok vertikal (720x1280 di render,
atau render landscape lalu crop vertikal di CapCut).

## Kunci karakter (COPY-PASTE PERSIS di semua scene)

> **OLIVIA, wanita 28 tahun, rambut coklat panjang, gaun merah marun, kalung emas tipis.**
> **RYAN, pria 32 tahun, suami Olivia, jas navy, rambut hitam rapi, jam tangan mahal.**
> **JAKE, pria 33 tahun, suami Mia, kemeja putih lengan digulung, rambut coklat gelap, tatapan tenang, jam kulit hitam.**
> **MIA, wanita 29 tahun, sahabat Olivia, istri Jake, rambut pirang pendek, gaun hitam.**

Foto referensi: buat 1 foto tiap karakter dulu (Dreamina / Gemini image),
upload foto itu sebagai gambar awal di tiap scene (image-to-video).
Jangan ganti deskripsi di tengah episode.

## Setting global

- Negative prompt (tempel di semua scene):
  `blurry, low quality, distorted face, extra fingers, deformed hands, watermark, text, logo`
- Kamera: slow push-in / close-up, jangan pakai gerakan kamera ekstrem di T4 480p.
- Dialog: Wan 2.2 5B tidak bisa diandalkan untuk dialog Indonesia yang jelas.
  Render **tanpa suara dulu**, dubbing belakangan (CapCut TTS / ElevenLabs free),
  lalu tempel subtitle bakar di edit.

## Scene 1 — Ryan Menunggu di Balkon (0–5 detik)

**Visual:** Dini hari, balkon vila menghadap danau. Ryan berdiri sendiri, menatap air yang gelap.

**Prompt:**
> Ryan, man 32 years old, Olivia's husband, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch,
> standing alone on a villa balcony facing a dark lake at dawn, looking down at the water,
> regret on his face, cold morning light, camera slow push-in, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Aku yang membawa Olivia ke tempat ini. Ini salahku."

## Scene 2 — Olivia Menghadapi Ryan (5–10 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia keluar ke balkon, berdiri di belakang Ryan, menatap punggungnya dengan marah sekaligus ragu.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, woman 28 years old, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> standing behind Ryan, man 32 years old, navy suit, neat black hair, on a villa balcony at dawn,
> facing his back, angry but hesitant, lake and mist behind them, camera medium shot static

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Jake dikurung di bawah vila. Kamu tahu, kan?"

## Scene 3 — Ryan Mengaku (10–15 detik)

**Visual:** Ryan berbalik, wajahnya penuh penyesalan. Ia menunjukkan kunci besi kecil di tangannya.

**Prompt:**
> Ryan, man 32 years old, Olivia's husband, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch,
> turning to face Olivia, woman 28 years old, long brown hair, maroon red dress,
> holding out a small iron key in his hand, remorseful expression, dawn light on the balcony, camera close-up

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Aku tidak tahu mereka akan mengurungnya. Kunci ini... aku yang mencurinya semalam."

## Scene 4 — Olivia Ragu (15–20 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia menatap kunci di tangan Ryan, lalu menatap mata Ryan, mencari tahu apakah ini jebakan lagi.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, woman 28 years old, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> staring at a small iron key in Ryan's hand, then looking up into Ryan's eyes,
> searching for the truth, torn between trust and doubt, camera close-up, soft dawn light

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Kenapa aku harus percaya padamu sekarang?"

## Scene 5 — Janji Ryan (20–25 detik)

**Visual:** Ryan meletakkan kunci di tangan Olivia, lalu menunduk. Olivia menggenggam kunci itu erat.

**Prompt:**
> Ryan, man 32 years old, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch,
> placing a small iron key into Olivia's hand, woman 28 years old, long brown hair, maroon red dress,
> bowing his head, Olivia closing her fingers tightly around the key,
> balcony at dawn, lake behind them, camera medium shot slowly moving closer, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Karena kali ini, aku akan membantumu menyelamatkannya. Bukan untukku — untukmu."

## Scene 6 — Ending / Cliffhanger (25–30 detik)

**Visual:** Close-up tangan Olivia menggenggam kunci besi, lalu layar menghitam. Teks: "Bersambung..."

**Prompt:**
> Close-up of Olivia's hand, woman 28 years old, thin gold necklace visible,
> tightly holding a small iron key, dawn light on her fingers, dark background,
> slowly fading to black, dramatic, cinematic, sharp detail

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Episode 36 — rencana penyelamatan dimulai."
