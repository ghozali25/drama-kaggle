# Episode 38 — Topeng Terbuka (6 Scene × 5 detik)

Format: Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B, image-to-video, 480p, 24 fps.
Total: ~30 detik. Cocok untuk Shorts / Reels / TikTok vertikal (720x1280 di render,
atau render landscape lalu crop vertikal di CapCut).

## Kunci karakter (COPY-PASTE PERSIS di semua scene)

> **OLIVIA, wanita 28 tahun, rambut coklat panjang, gaun merah marun, kalung emas tipis.**
> **RYAN, pria 32 tahun, suami Olivia, jas navy, rambut hitam rapi, jam tangan mahal.**
> **JAKE, pria 33 tahun, suami Mia, kemeja putih lengan digulung, rambut coklat gelap, tatapan tenang, jam kulit hitam.**
> **MIA, wanita 29 tahun, sahabat Olivia, istri Jake, rambut pirang pendek, gaun hitam.**

Foto referensi: buat 1 foto tiap karakter dulu (Dreamina / Gemini image),
upload foto itu sebagai gambar awal di tiap scene (image-to-video).
Jangan ganti deskripsi di tengah episode.

## Setting global

- Negative prompt (tempel di semua scene):
  `blurry, low quality, distorted face, extra fingers, deformed hands, watermark, text, logo`
- Kamera: slow push-in / close-up, jangan pakai gerakan kamera ekstrem di T4 480p.
- Dialog: Wan 2.2 5B tidak bisa diandalkan untuk dialog Indonesia yang jelas.
  Render **tanpa suara dulu**, dubbing belakangan (CapCut TTS / ElevenLabs free),
  lalu tempel subtitle bakar di edit.

## Scene 1 — Jake Keluar dari Sel (0–5 detik)

**Visual:** Jake melangkah keluar dari sel, Olivia menahan tangannya. Ryan berjaga di ujung koridor.

**Prompt:**
> Jake, man 33 years old, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch,
> stepping out of a dark basement cell, Olivia, woman 28 years old, long brown hair, maroon red dress,
> holding his arm, Ryan, man 32 years old, navy suit, watching the corridor,
> tense faces, dim light, camera medium shot, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Kita belum aman. Pemilik vila pasti sudah tahu."

## Scene 2 — Lampu Menyala Serentak (5–10 detik)

**Visual:** Tiba-tiba semua lampu koridor menyala. Di ujung koridor, sosok pria tua bertopeng emas berdiri bersama penjaga.

**Prompt:**
> An elderly man in an elegant suit wearing a gold mask,
> standing at the end of a brightly lit villa basement corridor at night,
> flanked by guards, Olivia, Jake, and Ryan facing him in shock,
> dramatic light, camera wide shot slowly moving closer, tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Kalian pikir bisa keluar dari vilaku semudah itu?"

## Scene 3 — Pria Bertopeng Mendekat (10–15 detik)

**Visual:** Pria bertopeng emas berjalan perlahan mendekat, menatap Jake lekat-lekat. Jake membeku.

**Prompt:**
> An elderly man in an elegant suit wearing a gold mask,
> walking slowly toward Jake, man 33 years old, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze,
> in a villa basement corridor, Jake frozen in shock, Olivia stepping in front of him,
> camera slow push-in, dramatic, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Wajah itu... aku mengenal wajah itu."

## Scene 4 — Topeng Dilepas (15–20 detik)

**Visual:** Pria itu melepas topeng emasnya perlahan. Wajah tua dengan sorot mata yang sama seperti Jake.

**Prompt:**
> An elderly man in an elegant suit slowly removing a gold mask in a villa basement corridor,
> revealing an old face with the same eyes as Jake, man 33 years old, dark brown hair, calm gaze,
> Jake staring in disbelief, Olivia gasping, camera close-up, dramatic light

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Tidak mungkin... Ayah?"

## Scene 5 — Jake Menatap Ayahnya (20–25 detik)

**Visual:** Jake dan ayahnya berdiri berhadapan, jarak beberapa langkah. Olivia dan Ryan menonton dari samping, tak berani bergerak.

**Prompt:**
> Jake, man 33 years old, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch,
> standing face to face with his elderly father in an elegant suit in a villa basement corridor,
> both staring at each other, years of silence between them,
> Olivia and Ryan watching from the side, camera medium shot, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Ayah yang dulu mengusirku... ternyata pemilik vila ini."

## Scene 6 — Ending / Cliffhanger (25–30 detik)

**Visual:** Close-up mata ayah Jake yang dingin, lalu layar menghitam. Teks: "Bersambung..."

**Prompt:**
> Close-up of an elderly man's cold eyes in a villa basement corridor,
> the same calm gaze as Jake, dramatic light, dark background,
> slowly fading to black, dramatic, cinematic, sharp eye detail

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Episode 39 — kenapa ayah Jake membuat retreat ini?"
