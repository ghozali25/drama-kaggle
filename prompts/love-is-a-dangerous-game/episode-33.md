# Episode 33 — Pertukaran (6 Scene × 5 detik)

Format: Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B, image-to-video, 480p, 24 fps.
Total: ~30 detik. Cocok untuk Shorts / Reels / TikTok vertikal (720x1280 di render,
atau render landscape lalu crop vertikal di CapCut).

## Kunci karakter (COPY-PASTE PERSIS di semua scene)

> **OLIVIA, wanita 28 tahun, rambut coklat panjang, gaun merah marun, kalung emas tipis.**
> **RYAN, pria 32 tahun, suami Olivia, jas navy, rambut hitam rapi, jam tangan mahal.**
> **JAKE, pria 33 tahun, suami Mia, kemeja putih lengan digulung, rambut coklat gelap, tatapan tenang, jam kulit hitam.**
> **MIA, wanita 29 tahun, sahabat Olivia, istri Jake, rambut pirang pendek, gaun hitam.**

Foto referensi: buat 1 foto Olivia + 1 foto Ryan + 1 foto Jake + 1 foto Mia dulu (Dreamina / Gemini image),
upload foto itu sebagai gambar awal di tiap scene (image-to-video).
Jangan ganti deskripsi di tengah episode.

## Setting global

- Negative prompt (tempel di semua scene):
  `blurry, low quality, distorted face, extra fingers, deformed hands, watermark, text, logo`
- Kamera: slow push-in / close-up, jangan pakai gerakan kamera ekstrem di T4 480p.
- Dialog: Wan 2.2 5B tidak bisa diandalkan untuk dialog Indonesia yang jelas.
  Render **tanpa suara dulu**, dubbing belakangan (CapCut TTS / ElevenLabs free),
  lalu tempel subtitle bakar di edit.

## Scene 1 — Hook (0–5 detik)

**Visual:** Pagi hari di aula utama vila. Olivia melangkah maju membawa sebuah map, sementara Mia masih dipegangi pengawal.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> stepping forward in a villa hall in the morning carrying a folder, while Mia, 29-year-old woman, short blonde hair, black dress,
> is still held by guards, camera slow push-in, cinematic, tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Aku serahkan kontraknya. Tapi lepaskan Mia dulu."

## Scene 2 — Kontrak Palsu (5–10 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia menyerahkan map itu kepada pemilik vila. Di dalam map itu sebenarnya kontrak palsu yang dibuat Jake semalam, terlihat sama persis dari luar.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, handing a folder to an old villa owner,
> the folder actually containing a fake contract made by Jake, 33-year-old man, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves,
> looking identical from the outside, villa hall, camera close-up, tense, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Ini kontraknya. Periksa sendiri kalau tidak percaya."

## Scene 3 — Mia Dilepaskan (10–15 detik)

**Visual:** Pemilik vila memberi isyarat, pengawal melepaskan Mia. Mia langsung berlari ke pelukan Olivia, menangis lega.

**Prompt:**
> Mia, 29-year-old woman, short blonde hair, black dress, being released by guards and running into Olivia's arms,
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace, hugging her as Mia cries with relief,
> villa hall, morning light, camera medium shot, emotional

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Mia selamat. Rencana kita berhasil."

## Scene 4 — Pemilik Vila Curiga (15–20 detik)

**Visual:** Pemilik vila membuka map itu dan membaca cepat, wajahnya berubah curiga. Ia menatap Jake dengan tajam.

**Prompt:**
> An old villa owner opening the folder and reading quickly, his face turning suspicious,
> staring sharply at Jake, 33-year-old man, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch,
> villa hall, morning light, camera close-up, tense, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Ini bukan yang asli. Kamu menipuku, Jake."

## Scene 5 — Jake Ditangkap (20–25 detik)

**Visual:** Dua pengawal menangkap lengan Jake dan menariknya pergi. Olivia berteriak dan mencoba mengejar, tapi Ryan menahannya.

**Prompt:**
> Two guards grabbing Jake's arms, 33-year-old man, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze,
> and dragging him away, Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress,
> shouting and trying to chase him while Ryan, 32-year-old man, navy suit, holds her back,
> villa hall, camera medium shot, dramatic, tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Jake! Lepaskan dia! Kontrak aslinya ada padaku, bukan padanya!"

## Scene 6 — Ending / Cliffhanger (25–30 detik)

**Visual:** Close-up mata Jake yang tenang meski ditangkap, lalu layar menghitam. Teks: "Bersambung..."

**Prompt:**
> Close-up of Jake's calm eyes, 33-year-old man, dark brown hair, calm gaze, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves,
> staring at the camera even as he is taken away, dark background, slowly fading to black,
> dramatic, cinematic, sharp eye detail

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Jaga kontrak aslinya, Olivia. Aku akan kembali."
