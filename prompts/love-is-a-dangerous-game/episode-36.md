# Episode 36 — Rencana Penyelamatan (6 Scene × 5 detik)

Format: Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B, image-to-video, 480p, 24 fps.
Total: ~30 detik. Cocok untuk Shorts / Reels / TikTok vertikal (720x1280 di render,
atau render landscape lalu crop vertikal di CapCut).

## Kunci karakter (COPY-PASTE PERSIS di semua scene)

> **OLIVIA, wanita 28 tahun, rambut coklat panjang, gaun merah marun, kalung emas tipis.**
> **RYAN, pria 32 tahun, suami Olivia, jas navy, rambut hitam rapi, jam tangan mahal.**
> **JAKE, pria 33 tahun, suami Mia, kemeja putih lengan digulung, rambut coklat gelap, tatapan tenang, jam kulit hitam.**
> **MIA, wanita 29 tahun, sahabat Olivia, istri Jake, rambut pirang pendek, gaun hitam.**

Foto referensi: buat 1 foto tiap karakter dulu (Dreamina / Gemini image),
upload foto itu sebagai gambar awal di tiap scene (image-to-video).
Jangan ganti deskripsi di tengah episode.

## Setting global

- Negative prompt (tempel di semua scene):
  `blurry, low quality, distorted face, extra fingers, deformed hands, watermark, text, logo`
- Kamera: slow push-in / close-up, jangan pakai gerakan kamera ekstrem di T4 480p.
- Dialog: Wan 2.2 5B tidak bisa diandalkan untuk dialog Indonesia yang jelas.
  Render **tanpa suara dulu**, dubbing belakangan (CapCut TTS / ElevenLabs free),
  lalu tempel subtitle bakar di edit.

## Scene 1 — Berkumpul di Kamar (0–5 detik)

**Visual:** Siang hari, kamar Olivia. Olivia, Ryan, dan Mia duduk mengelilingi meja kecil, berbisik serius.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, woman 28 years old, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> Ryan, man 32 years old, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch,
> and Mia, woman 29 years old, short blonde hair, black dress,
> sitting around a small table in a villa bedroom in daylight, whispering seriously,
> curtains half closed, camera medium shot static, tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Kita hanya punya satu kesempatan. Besok malam, saat pesta topeng."

## Scene 2 — Mia Menggambar Denah (5–10 detik)

**Visual:** Mia menggambar denah bawah vila di atas kertas, menunjuk jalur dari dapur ke sel Jake.

**Prompt:**
> Mia, woman 29 years old, short blonde hair, black dress,
> drawing a floor plan of the villa basement on paper at a small table,
> pointing at a route from the kitchen to the cell, focused face,
> Olivia and Ryan watching, daylight through curtains, camera close-up on the paper

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Dari dapur, ada tangga kecil. Itu jalan yang Olivia temukan."

## Scene 3 — Ryan Menjelaskan Penjaga (10–15 detik)

**Visual:** Ryan menunjuk jam tangannya, menjelaskan jadwal pergantian penjaga di koridor bawah.

**Prompt:**
> Ryan, man 32 years old, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch,
> pointing at his expensive watch while explaining to Olivia and Mia in a villa bedroom,
> serious expression, hand gestures showing guard shift times,
> camera medium shot, daylight, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Penjaga berganti tiap dua jam. Celahnya hanya sepuluh menit."

## Scene 4 — Olivia Membagi Tugas (15–20 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia menatap Mia dan Ryan bergantian, membagi tugas: Mia mengalihkan perhatian, Ryan membuka pintu, Olivia membawa Jake keluar.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, woman 28 years old, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> looking between Mia, woman 29 years old, short blonde hair, black dress,
> and Ryan, man 32 years old, navy suit, giving instructions in a villa bedroom,
> determined face, camera close-up, soft daylight

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Mia di aula, Ryan bersamaku di bawah. Tidak ada yang bertindak sendiri."

## Scene 5 — Tiga Tangan Bertumpuk (20–25 detik)

**Visual:** Ketiganya menumpukkan tangan di atas meja, sebagai janji. Wajah mereka tegang tapi bersatu.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, woman 28 years old, long brown hair, maroon red dress,
> Ryan, man 32 years old, navy suit,
> and Mia, woman 29 years old, short blonde hair, black dress,
> stacking their hands together over a small table in a villa bedroom,
> determined faces, daylight, camera low angle slowly moving closer, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Besok malam, kita bebaskan Jake. Apa pun yang terjadi."

## Scene 6 — Ending / Cliffhanger (25–30 detik)

**Visual:** Close-up kertas denah yang dilipat Olivia, lalu layar menghitam. Teks: "Bersambung..."

**Prompt:**
> Close-up of a folded paper floor plan held by Olivia's hand, woman 28 years old, thin gold necklace visible,
> folding the paper carefully in a villa bedroom, daylight fading,
> slowly fading to black, dramatic, cinematic, sharp detail

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Episode 37 — penyusupan saat pesta topeng."
