# Episode 34 — Penjara Bawah Tanah (6 Scene × 5 detik)

Format: Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B, image-to-video, 480p, 24 fps.
Total: ~30 detik. Cocok untuk Shorts / Reels / TikTok vertikal (720x1280 di render,
atau render landscape lalu crop vertikal di CapCut).

## Kunci karakter (COPY-PASTE PERSIS di semua scene)

> **OLIVIA, wanita 28 tahun, rambut coklat panjang, gaun merah marun, kalung emas tipis.**
> **RYAN, pria 32 tahun, suami Olivia, jas navy, rambut hitam rapi, jam tangan mahal.**
> **JAKE, pria 33 tahun, suami Mia, kemeja putih lengan digulung, rambut coklat gelap, tatapan tenang, jam kulit hitam.**
> **MIA, wanita 29 tahun, sahabat Olivia, istri Jake, rambut pirang pendek, gaun hitam.**

Foto referensi: buat 1 foto tiap karakter dulu (Dreamina / Gemini image),
upload foto itu sebagai gambar awal di tiap scene (image-to-video).
Jangan ganti deskripsi di tengah episode.

## Setting global

- Negative prompt (tempel di semua scene):
  `blurry, low quality, distorted face, extra fingers, deformed hands, watermark, text, logo`
- Kamera: slow push-in / close-up, jangan pakai gerakan kamera ekstrem di T4 480p.
- Dialog: Wan 2.2 5B tidak bisa diandalkan untuk dialog Indonesia yang jelas.
  Render **tanpa suara dulu**, dubbing belakangan (CapCut TTS / ElevenLabs free),
  lalu tempel subtitle bakar di edit.

## Scene 1 — Pintu Terkunci (0–5 detik)

**Visual:** Malam hari, koridor bawah vila yang gelap. Olivia menempelkan telinga ke pintu besi yang terkunci.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, woman 28 years old, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> pressing her ear against a locked iron door in a dark basement corridor of a lakeside villa at night,
> dim cold light, tense face, listening carefully, camera slow push-in, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Jake... kamu di dalam sana, kan? Jawab aku!"

## Scene 2 — Suara dari Balik Pintu (5–10 detik)

**Visual:** Di balik jeruji kecil pintu besi, Jake duduk bersandar di dinding, wajahnya tenang meski bajunya kotor.

**Prompt:**
> Jake, man 33 years old, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch,
> sitting against a stone wall inside a dark basement cell, looking up through a small barred window in the iron door,
> faint light on his face, calm but tired, camera close-up, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Olivia, jangan berisik. Mereka mengawasi koridor ini."

## Scene 3 — Penjaga Datang (10–15 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia bersembunyi di balik pilar saat dua penjaga vila lewat membawa lentera.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, woman 28 years old, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> hiding behind a stone pillar in a dark villa basement as two guards walk past carrying lanterns,
> holding her breath, fear in her eyes, camera low angle, slow movement, tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Mereka tidak boleh tahu aku di sini."

## Scene 4 — Pesan Lewat Celah Pintu (15–20 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia menyelipkan selembar kertas kecil lewat celah bawah pintu besi. Jake mengambilnya.

**Prompt:**
> Jake, man 33 years old, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch,
> picking up a small folded paper slipped under the iron cell door in a dark basement,
> reading it by faint light, eyes narrowing with focus, camera close-up

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Aku akan keluar. Tapi bukan malam ini — besok, saat pesta topeng."

## Scene 5 — Olivia Menemukan Jalan Masuk (20–25 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia menemukan pintu kecil berkarat di ujung koridor, di baliknya ada tangga sempit menuju dapur vila.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, woman 28 years old, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> discovering a small rusted door at the end of a dark basement corridor, a narrow staircase behind it leading up,
> touching the rusted handle, determined face, camera medium shot slowly moving closer, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Ini jalannya. Aku akan kembali membawa bantuan."

## Scene 6 — Ending / Cliffhanger (25–30 detik)

**Visual:** Close-up mata Jake di balik jeruji, lalu layar menghitam. Teks: "Bersambung..."

**Prompt:**
> Close-up of Jake's eyes, man 33 years old, dark brown hair, calm gaze,
> looking through iron bars in a dark basement cell, determined stare, background dark,
> slowly fading to black, dramatic, cinematic, sharp eye detail

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Episode 35 — Ryan akhirnya memilih sisi yang mana?"
