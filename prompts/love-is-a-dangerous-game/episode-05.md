# Episode 05 — Permainan Kartu (6 Scene × 5 detik)

Format: Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B, image-to-video, 480p, 24 fps.
Total: ~30 detik. Cocok untuk Shorts / Reels / TikTok vertikal (720x1280 di render,
atau render landscape lalu crop vertikal di CapCut).

## Kunci karakter (COPY-PASTE PERSIS di semua scene)

> **OLIVIA, wanita 28 tahun, rambut coklat panjang, gaun merah marun, kalung emas tipis.**
> **RYAN, pria 32 tahun, suami Olivia, jas navy, rambut hitam rapi, jam tangan mahal.**
> **JAKE, pria 33 tahun, suami Mia, kemeja putih lengan digulung, rambut coklat gelap, tatapan tenang, jam kulit hitam.**
> **MIA, wanita 29 tahun, sahabat Olivia, istri Jake, rambut pirang pendek, gaun hitam.**

Foto referensi: buat 1 foto Olivia + 1 foto Ryan + 1 foto Jake + 1 foto Mia dulu
(Dreamina / Gemini image), upload foto itu sebagai gambar awal di tiap scene
(image-to-video). Jangan ganti deskripsi di tengah episode.

## Setting global

- Negative prompt (tempel di semua scene):
  `blurry, low quality, distorted face, extra fingers, deformed hands, watermark, text, logo`
- Kamera: slow push-in / close-up, jangan pakai gerakan kamera ekstrem di T4 480p.
- Dialog: Wan 2.2 5B tidak bisa diandalkan untuk dialog Indonesia yang jelas.
  Render **tanpa suara dulu**, dubbing belakangan (CapCut TTS / ElevenLabs free),
  lalu tempel subtitle bakar di edit.

## Scene 1 — Hook (0–5 detik)

**Visual:** Malam hari, ruang santai vila. Semua pasangan duduk melingkar. Tuan rumah meletakkan setumpuk kartu pertanyaan di tengah meja.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace, Ryan,
> 32-year-old man, Olivia's husband, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch, Jake,
> 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze,
> black leather watch, and Mia, 29-year-old woman, Olivia's best friend, Jake's wife,
> short blonde hair, black dress, sitting in a circle in a villa lounge at night,
> a stack of question cards in the middle of the table, warm lamp light,
> camera wide shot slowly pushing in, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Permainan kejujuran. Yang berbohong, kalah."

## Scene 2 — Giliran Olivia (5–10 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia mengambil kartu dan membacanya: "Apa rahasia kecil yang belum pernah kamu ceritakan pada pasanganmu?" Ia menelan ludah.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace,
> drawing a question card and reading it, swallowing nervously, sitting in a circle
> in a villa lounge at night, warm lamp light, camera close-up on the card, slow push-in

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Apa rahasia kecil yang belum pernah kamu ceritakan pada pasanganmu?"

## Scene 3 — Jake Menjawab (10–15 detik)

**Visual:** Sebelum Olivia menjawab, Jake berkata pelan: ia tahu Olivia menyimpan surat lama di laci kamarnya. Olivia terkejut.

**Prompt:**
> Jake, 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled sleeves, dark brown hair,
> calm gaze, black leather watch, speaking quietly in a villa lounge at night, Olivia,
> 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace, looking shocked,
> warm lamp light, camera close-up, suspenseful

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Kamu menyimpan surat lama di laci kamarmu. Surat yang tidak pernah kamu kirim."

## Scene 4 — Ryan Terkejut (15–20 detik)

**Visual:** Ryan menoleh tajam ke arah Olivia. Ia tidak tahu soal surat itu. Mia menunduk, menghindari tatapan siapa pun.

**Prompt:**
> Ryan, 32-year-old man, Olivia's husband, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch,
> turning sharply toward Olivia, surprised, not knowing about the letter, Mia,
> 29-year-old woman, Olivia's best friend, Jake's wife, short blonde hair, black dress,
> looking down, avoiding everyone's gaze, villa lounge at night, camera medium shot, tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Surat apa? Kenapa aku tidak pernah tahu?"

## Scene 5 — Dari Mana Jake Tahu (20–25 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia bertanya dari mana Jake tahu. Jake hanya tersenyum tipis dan mengocok kartu berikutnya, tidak menjawab.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace,
> asking Jake how he knows, Jake, 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled sleeves,
> dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch, smiling faintly and shuffling the next card,
> not answering, villa lounge at night, camera close-up, slow push-in

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Dari mana kamu tahu soal surat itu, Jake?"

## Scene 6 — Ending / Cliffhanger (25–30 detik)

**Visual:** Close-up kartu terakhir di tangan Jake — di belakang kartu itu tertulis nama "OLIVIA" dengan tinta merah. Layar menghitam. Teks: "Bersambung..."

**Prompt:**
> Close-up of the last question card in Jake's hand, 33-year-old man, Mia's husband,
> white shirt with rolled sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch,
> the name "OLIVIA" written in red ink on the back of the card, dim lamp light,
> slowly fading to black, dramatic, cinematic, sharp detail

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Episode 6 — kartu itu memang untukku sejak awal?"
