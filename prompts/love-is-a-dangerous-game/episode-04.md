# Episode 04 — Makan Malam Pertama (6 Scene × 5 detik)

Format: Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B, image-to-video, 480p, 24 fps.
Total: ~30 detik. Cocok untuk Shorts / Reels / TikTok vertikal (720x1280 di render,
atau render landscape lalu crop vertikal di CapCut).

## Kunci karakter (COPY-PASTE PERSIS di semua scene)

> **OLIVIA, wanita 28 tahun, rambut coklat panjang, gaun merah marun, kalung emas tipis.**
> **RYAN, pria 32 tahun, suami Olivia, jas navy, rambut hitam rapi, jam tangan mahal.**
> **JAKE, pria 33 tahun, suami Mia, kemeja putih lengan digulung, rambut coklat gelap, tatapan tenang, jam kulit hitam.**
> **MIA, wanita 29 tahun, sahabat Olivia, istri Jake, rambut pirang pendek, gaun hitam.**

Foto referensi: buat 1 foto Olivia + 1 foto Ryan + 1 foto Jake + 1 foto Mia dulu
(Dreamina / Gemini image), upload foto itu sebagai gambar awal di tiap scene
(image-to-video). Jangan ganti deskripsi di tengah episode.

## Setting global

- Negative prompt (tempel di semua scene):
  `blurry, low quality, distorted face, extra fingers, deformed hands, watermark, text, logo`
- Kamera: slow push-in / close-up, jangan pakai gerakan kamera ekstrem di T4 480p.
- Dialog: Wan 2.2 5B tidak bisa diandalkan untuk dialog Indonesia yang jelas.
  Render **tanpa suara dulu**, dubbing belakangan (CapCut TTS / ElevenLabs free),
  lalu tempel subtitle bakar di edit.

## Scene 1 — Hook (0–5 detik)

**Visual:** Malam hari, meja makan panjang di ruang makan vila. Olivia duduk di samping Jake, Ryan duduk di seberang bersama Mia.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace,
> sitting at a long dinner table in a villa dining room at night, next to Jake,
> 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled sleeves, dark brown hair,
> calm gaze, black leather watch, Ryan, 32-year-old man, Olivia's husband, navy suit,
> neat black hair, expensive watch, sitting across the table with Mia, 29-year-old woman,
> Olivia's best friend, Jake's wife, short blonde hair, black dress, candlelight,
> camera wide shot slowly pushing in, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Makan malam pertama dengan pasangan yang salah."

## Scene 2 — Jake yang Tenang (5–10 detik)

**Visual:** Jake menuangkan air untuk Olivia dengan tenang, tidak canggung sama sekali. Olivia memperhatikannya diam-diam.

**Prompt:**
> Jake, 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled sleeves, dark brown hair,
> calm gaze, black leather watch, calmly pouring water for Olivia, 28-year-old woman,
> long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace, Olivia watching him quietly,
> candlelight dinner table, camera medium shot static, calm and tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Kamu tidak gugup sama sekali, ya?"

## Scene 3 — Ryan Cemburu (10–15 detik)

**Visual:** Ryan menatap Jake dan Olivia dari seberang meja, rahangnya mengeras, gelasnya ia genggam terlalu kuat.

**Prompt:**
> Ryan, 32-year-old man, Olivia's husband, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch,
> staring across the dinner table at Jake and Olivia, jaw clenched, gripping his glass too tightly,
> Mia, 29-year-old woman, Olivia's best friend, Jake's wife, short blonde hair, black dress,
> sitting beside him, candlelight, camera close-up, slow push-in

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Aku suaminya. Bukan dia."

## Scene 4 — Mia Menutupi (15–20 detik)

**Visual:** Mia tertawa keras dan mengalihkan pembicaraan saat Olivia bertanya kenapa Jake begitu tenang. Tawanya terdengar dipaksakan.

**Prompt:**
> Mia, 29-year-old woman, Olivia's best friend, Jake's wife, short blonde hair, black dress,
> laughing loudly and changing the subject at a candlelight dinner table, her laugh forced,
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace,
> watching her suspiciously, camera medium shot, slow push-in

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Jake memang begitu. Dia tenang di mana-mana. Sudahlah, makan saja."

## Scene 5 — Pertanyaan Jake (20–25 detik)

**Visual:** Jake bertanya pelan pada Olivia, apakah Ryan sering memaksanya datang ke acara yang tidak ia sukai. Olivia terdiam.

**Prompt:**
> Jake, 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled sleeves, dark brown hair,
> calm gaze, black leather watch, asking Olivia a quiet question at a candlelight dinner table,
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace,
> falling silent, looking down at her plate, camera close-up, suspenseful

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Ryan sering memaksamu datang ke tempat yang tidak kamu mau?"

## Scene 6 — Ending / Cliffhanger (25–30 detik)

**Visual:** Close-up tangan Ryan yang meremas serbet di bawah meja sampai berkerut. Layar menghitam. Teks: "Bersambung..."

**Prompt:**
> Close-up of Ryan's hand, 32-year-old man, Olivia's husband, navy suit, neat black hair,
> expensive watch, crumpling a napkin under the dinner table, candlelight, slowly fading to black,
> dramatic, cinematic, sharp detail

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Episode 5 — permainan apa yang menunggu kita?"
