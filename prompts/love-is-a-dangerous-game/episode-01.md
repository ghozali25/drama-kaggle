# Episode 01 — Undangan (6 Scene × 5 detik)

Format: Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B, image-to-video, 480p, 24 fps.
Total: ~30 detik. Cocok untuk Shorts / Reels / TikTok vertikal (720x1280 di render,
atau render landscape lalu crop vertikal di CapCut).

## Kunci karakter (COPY-PASTE PERSIS di semua scene)

> **OLIVIA, wanita 28 tahun, rambut coklat panjang, gaun merah marun, kalung emas tipis.**
> **RYAN, pria 32 tahun, suami Olivia, jas navy, rambut hitam rapi, jam tangan mahal.**
> **JAKE, pria 33 tahun, suami Mia, kemeja putih lengan digulung, rambut coklat gelap, tatapan tenang, jam kulit hitam.**
> **MIA, wanita 29 tahun, sahabat Olivia, istri Jake, rambut pirang pendek, gaun hitam.**

Foto referensi: buat 1 foto Olivia + 1 foto Ryan + 1 foto Jake + 1 foto Mia dulu
(Dreamina / Gemini image), upload foto itu sebagai gambar awal di tiap scene
(image-to-video). Jangan ganti deskripsi di tengah episode.

## Setting global

- Negative prompt (tempel di semua scene):
  `blurry, low quality, distorted face, extra fingers, deformed hands, watermark, text, logo`
- Kamera: slow push-in / close-up, jangan pakai gerakan kamera ekstrem di T4 480p.
- Dialog: Wan 2.2 5B tidak bisa diandalkan untuk dialog Indonesia yang jelas.
  Render **tanpa suara dulu**, dubbing belakangan (CapCut TTS / ElevenLabs free),
  lalu tempel subtitle bakar di edit.

## Scene 1 — Hook (0–5 detik)

**Visual:** Malam hari, ruang tamu rumah Olivia dan Ryan. Olivia memegang undangan emas, menatap marah ke arah Ryan.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace,
> holding a gold invitation card in a living room at night, staring angrily at Ryan,
> 32-year-old man, Olivia's husband, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch,
> standing across the room, tense atmosphere, camera close-up slowly pushing in, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Kamu pikir aku akan ikut begitu saja?"

## Scene 2 — Undangan Retreat (5–10 detik)

**Visual:** Ryan meletakkan undangan di meja, tersenyum tipis. Olivia membaca tulisan "Retreat Pasangan — Vila Danau".

**Prompt:**
> Ryan, 32-year-old man, Olivia's husband, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch,
> placing a gold invitation on a table, smiling faintly, Olivia, 28-year-old woman,
> long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace, reading the invitation,
> warm lamp light, camera medium shot static, suspenseful

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Ini cuma liburan pasangan. Mia dan Jake juga datang."

## Scene 3 — Kecurigaan (10–15 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia membalik undangan, menemukan tidak ada nama penyelenggara. Wajahnya tegang.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace,
> turning over a gold invitation card, no organizer name on the back,
> her face tense and suspicious, dim living room light, camera close-up, slow push-in

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Kenapa tidak ada nama siapa yang mengundang kita?"

## Scene 4 — Desakan Ryan (15–20 detik)

**Visual:** Ryan mendekat, memegang bahu Olivia, tatapannya dingin. Olivia menepis tangannya pelan.

**Prompt:**
> Ryan, 32-year-old man, Olivia's husband, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch,
> stepping closer and holding Olivia's shoulder, cold stare, Olivia, 28-year-old woman,
> long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace, pushing his hand away gently,
> camera low angle, slow movement, tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Aku sudah bilang ya. Jangan buat aku malu di depan mereka."

## Scene 5 — Telepon Mia (20–25 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia menelepon Mia di balkon. Mia di layar telepon tersenyum, tapi matanya gelisah.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace,
> calling on her phone on a balcony at night, Mia, 29-year-old woman, Olivia's best friend,
> Jake's wife, short blonde hair, black dress, smiling on the phone screen but her eyes nervous,
> city lights behind, camera medium shot slowly pushing in, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Mia, kamu yakin retreat ini aman?"

## Scene 6 — Ending / Cliffhanger (25–30 detik)

**Visual:** Close-up mata Olivia, lalu layar menghitam. Teks: "Bersambung..."

**Prompt:**
> Close-up of Olivia's eyes, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress,
> thin gold necklace, staring sharply at the camera, dark background,
> slowly fading to black, dramatic, cinematic, sharp eye detail

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Episode 2 — vila itu menyimpan apa?"
