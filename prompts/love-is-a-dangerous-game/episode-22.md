# Episode 22 — Di Tepi Jurang (6 Scene × 5 detik)

Format: Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B, image-to-video, 480p, 24 fps.
Total: ~30 detik. Cocok untuk Shorts / Reels / TikTok vertikal (720x1280 di render,
atau render landscape lalu crop vertikal di CapCut).

## Kunci karakter (COPY-PASTE PERSIS di semua scene)

> **OLIVIA, wanita 28 tahun, rambut coklat panjang, gaun merah marun, kalung emas tipis.**
> **RYAN, pria 32 tahun, suami Olivia, jas navy, rambut hitam rapi, jam tangan mahal.**
> **JAKE, pria 33 tahun, suami Mia, kemeja putih lengan digulung, rambut coklat gelap, tatapan tenang, jam kulit hitam.**
> **MIA, wanita 29 tahun, sahabat Olivia, istri Jake, rambut pirang pendek, gaun hitam.**

Foto referensi: buat 1 foto Olivia + 1 foto Ryan + 1 foto Jake + 1 foto Mia dulu
(Dreamina / Gemini image), upload foto itu sebagai gambar awal di tiap scene
(image-to-video). Jangan ganti deskripsi di tengah episode.

## Setting global

- Negative prompt (tempel di semua scene):
  `blurry, low quality, distorted face, extra fingers, deformed hands, watermark, text, logo`
- Kamera: slow push-in / close-up, jangan pakai gerakan kamera ekstrem di T4 480p.
- Dialog: Wan 2.2 5B tidak bisa diandalkan untuk dialog Indonesia yang jelas.
  Render **tanpa suara dulu**, dubbing belakangan (CapCut TTS / ElevenLabs free),
  lalu tempel subtitle bakar di edit.
- Lokasi: tepi jurang di ujung hutan tepi danau, tengah malam, bulan purnama, kabut, danau jauh di bawah.

## Scene 1 — Hook (0–5 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia berhenti di tepi jurang, tidak ada jalan lagi. Di belakangnya, Ryan dan Jake muncul dari kabut.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, a 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> stopping at the edge of a cliff at midnight, no way forward, moonlight and fog,
> Ryan, a 32-year-old man, Olivia's husband, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch, and Jake, a 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch, emerging from the fog behind her,
> wide shot, tense, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Tidak ada jalan lagi. Dan mereka berdua sudah di sini."

## Scene 2 — Ryan Memohon (5–10 detik)

**Visual:** Ryan mengulurkan tangan ke Olivia, memohon dia kembali, wajahnya penuh penyesalan.

**Prompt:**
> Ryan, a 32-year-old man, Olivia's husband, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch,
> reaching out his hand toward Olivia, a 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace, at the edge of a cliff at midnight, pleading,
> moonlight and fog, close-up, emotional, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Olivia, kembali padaku. Aku akan memperbaiki semuanya. Aku janji."

## Scene 3 — Jake Melangkah Maju (10–15 detik)

**Visual:** Jake melangkah maju melewati Ryan, berdiri di antara Olivia dan jurang, tatapannya berubah — tidak lagi sekadar tenang.

**Prompt:**
> Jake, a 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch,
> stepping forward past Ryan, a 32-year-old man, Olivia's husband, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch, standing between Olivia, a 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace, and the cliff edge at midnight,
> calm gaze turning resolute, moonlight and fog, medium shot, tense, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Mundur, Ryan. Mulai malam ini, kamu tidak berhak lagi mendekatinya."

## Scene 4 — Pengungkapan (15–20 detik)

**Visual:** Jake menatap Olivia, lalu membuka jam kulit hitamnya — di baliknya ada lambang keluarga pewaris vila.

**Prompt:**
> Jake, a 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch,
> opening his black leather watch to reveal a family crest inside, showing it to Olivia, a 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace, at the edge of a cliff at midnight,

> moonlight, close-up, dramatic, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Namaku bukan sekadar Jake. Aku pewaris keluarga pemilik vila ini — yang menyamar untuk menghentikan permainan ayahku."

## Scene 5 — Olivia Terkejut (20–25 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia menatap lambang itu, lalu menatap Jake — semua potongan teka-teki akhirnya tersambung.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, a 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Jadi selama ini... kamu bukan tamu. Kamu yang selama ini melindungiku."

## Scene 6 — Ending / Cliffhanger (25–30 detik)

**Visual:** Dari kejauhan terdengar suara helikopter dan lampu sorot menyapu hutan. Ketiganya menoleh, lalu layar menghitam. Teks: "Bersambung..."

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, a 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace, Jake, a 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch, and Ryan, a 32-year-old man, Olivia's husband, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch,

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Episode 23 — siapa yang mengirim helikopter itu?"
