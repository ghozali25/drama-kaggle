# Episode 32 — Pemilik Vila Menyerang (6 Scene × 5 detik)

Format: Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B, image-to-video, 480p, 24 fps.
Total: ~30 detik. Cocok untuk Shorts / Reels / TikTok vertikal (720x1280 di render,
atau render landscape lalu crop vertikal di CapCut).

## Kunci karakter (COPY-PASTE PERSIS di semua scene)

> **OLIVIA, wanita 28 tahun, rambut coklat panjang, gaun merah marun, kalung emas tipis.**
> **RYAN, pria 32 tahun, suami Olivia, jas navy, rambut hitam rapi, jam tangan mahal.**
> **JAKE, pria 33 tahun, suami Mia, kemeja putih lengan digulung, rambut coklat gelap, tatapan tenang, jam kulit hitam.**
> **MIA, wanita 29 tahun, sahabat Olivia, istri Jake, rambut pirang pendek, gaun hitam.**

Foto referensi: buat 1 foto Olivia + 1 foto Ryan + 1 foto Jake + 1 foto Mia dulu (Dreamina / Gemini image),
upload foto itu sebagai gambar awal di tiap scene (image-to-video).
Jangan ganti deskripsi di tengah episode.

## Setting global

- Negative prompt (tempel di semua scene):
  `blurry, low quality, distorted face, extra fingers, deformed hands, watermark, text, logo`
- Kamera: slow push-in / close-up, jangan pakai gerakan kamera ekstrem di T4 480p.
- Dialog: Wan 2.2 5B tidak bisa diandalkan untuk dialog Indonesia yang jelas.
  Render **tanpa suara dulu**, dubbing belakangan (CapCut TTS / ElevenLabs free),
  lalu tempel subtitle bakar di edit.

## Scene 1 — Hook (0–5 detik)

**Visual:** Pagi hari di aula utama vila. Pemilik vila berdiri di tengah ruangan, dua pengawalnya memegangi Mia yang ketakutan.

**Prompt:**
> An old villa owner standing in the middle of a villa hall in the morning, two guards holding Mia,
> 29-year-old woman, short blonde hair, black dress, looking frightened,
> camera slow push-in, cinematic, tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Olivia, kita bicara baik-baik. Atau sahabatmu ini yang membayar."

## Scene 2 — Olivia dan Jake Masuk (5–10 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia dan Jake masuk ke aula, terkejut melihat Mia disandera. Jake langsung maju selangkah, melindungi Olivia.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> and Jake, 33-year-old man, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch,
> entering a villa hall in shock to see Mia, 29-year-old woman, short blonde hair, black dress, held by guards,
> Jake stepping forward to shield Olivia, morning light, camera medium shot, tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Lepaskan Mia! Dia tidak ada hubungannya dengan kontrak itu!"

## Scene 3 — Tuntutan (10–15 detik)

**Visual:** Pemilik vila mengulurkan tangan, meminta kontrak asli. Mia menatap Olivia dengan mata memohon.

**Prompt:**
> An old villa owner holding out his hand demanding the original contract, Mia, 29-year-old woman, short blonde hair, black dress,
> looking at Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, with pleading eyes,
> villa hall, morning light, camera close-up, tense, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Serahkan kontraknya, Olivia. Kalau tidak, Mia tidak akan keluar dari vila ini."

## Scene 4 — Olivia Ragu (15–20 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia memegang map kontrak itu erat-erat, menatap bergantian ke arah Mia dan Jake. Jake mengangguk pelan padanya.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> clutching the contract folder tightly, looking back and forth at Mia, 29-year-old woman, short blonde hair, black dress,
> and Jake, 33-year-old man, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, calm gaze, nodding slowly at her,
> villa hall, camera close-up, emotional, tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Aku tidak bisa membiarkan Mia terluka karena aku."

## Scene 5 — Ryan Muncul (20–25 detik)

**Visual:** Ryan masuk dari pintu samping aula, wajahnya tegang. Ia menatap pemilik vila, lalu menatap Olivia.

**Prompt:**
> Ryan, 32-year-old man, Olivia's husband, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch,
> entering a villa hall from a side door with a tense face, looking at the old villa owner, then at Olivia,
> 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, morning light, camera slow push-in, tense, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Hentikan ini. Ini sudah terlalu jauh, bahkan untukku."

## Scene 6 — Ending / Cliffhanger (25–30 detik)

**Visual:** Close-up mata Mia yang ketakutan, lalu layar menghitam. Teks: "Bersambung..."

**Prompt:**
> Close-up of Mia's frightened eyes, 29-year-old woman, short blonde hair, black dress,
> staring at the camera, dark background, slowly fading to black,
> dramatic, cinematic, sharp eye detail

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Olivia, jangan serahkan yang asli. Aku mohon."
