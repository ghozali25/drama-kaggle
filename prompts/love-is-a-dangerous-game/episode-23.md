# Episode 23 — Pewaris (6 Scene × 5 detik)

Format: Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B, image-to-video, 480p, 24 fps.
Total: ~30 detik. Cocok untuk Shorts / Reels / TikTok vertikal (720x1280 di render,
atau render landscape lalu crop vertikal di CapCut).

## Kunci karakter (COPY-PASTE PERSIS di semua scene)

> **OLIVIA, wanita 28 tahun, rambut coklat panjang, gaun merah marun, kalung emas tipis.**
> **RYAN, pria 32 tahun, suami Olivia, jas navy, rambut hitam rapi, jam tangan mahal.**
> **JAKE, pria 33 tahun, suami Mia, kemeja putih lengan digulung, rambut coklat gelap, tatapan tenang, jam kulit hitam.**
> **MIA, wanita 29 tahun, sahabat Olivia, istri Jake, rambut pirang pendek, gaun hitam.**

Foto referensi: buat 1 foto Olivia + 1 foto Ryan + 1 foto Jake + 1 foto Mia dulu (Dreamina / Gemini image),
upload foto itu sebagai gambar awal di tiap scene (image-to-video).
Jangan ganti deskripsi di tengah episode.

## Setting global

- Negative prompt (tempel di semua scene):
  `blurry, low quality, distorted face, extra fingers, deformed hands, watermark, text, logo`
- Kamera: slow push-in / close-up, jangan pakai gerakan kamera ekstrem di T4 480p.
- Dialog: Wan 2.2 5B tidak bisa diandalkan untuk dialog Indonesia yang jelas.
  Render **tanpa suara dulu**, dubbing belakangan (CapCut TTS / ElevenLabs free),
  lalu tempel subtitle bakar di edit.

## Scene 1 — Hook (0–5 detik)

**Visual:** Malam hari di perpustakaan vila tepi danau. Jake berdiri di depan rak buku tua, menatap Olivia dengan tenang.

**Prompt:**
> Jake, 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch,
> standing in front of an old bookshelf in a lakeside villa library at night, looking calmly at Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> warm lamp light, tense silence, camera slow push-in, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Sebelum kamu bertanya lagi, dengarkan aku sampai selesai."

## Scene 2 — Pengakuan (5–10 detik)

**Visual:** Jake membuka sebuah kotak kayu kecil, di dalamnya ada foto lama dan kunci kuningan. Olivia menatap dengan terkejut.

**Prompt:**
> Jake, 33-year-old man, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch,
> opening a small wooden box with an old photo and a brass key inside, Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> looking shocked beside him, library at night, camera medium shot, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Aku bukan suami biasa. Aku pewaris keluarga yang memiliki vila ini."

## Scene 3 — Alasan Menyamar (10–15 detik)

**Visual:** Jake menunjuk jendela ke arah danau yang gelap. Olivia mengikuti arah pandangannya, wajahnya penuh tanya.

**Prompt:**
> Jake, 33-year-old man, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze,
> pointing toward the dark lake through a tall window, Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress,
> following his gaze with a questioning face, night, camera close-up, slow movement, tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Aku menyamar karena ada yang memakai nama keluargaku untuk menjebak orang."

## Scene 4 — Bukti Penyelidikan (15–20 detik)

**Visual:** Jake menunjukkan catatan dan foto-foto lama di atas meja kayu. Olivia memegang salah satu foto, tangannya gemetar.

**Prompt:**
> Jake, 33-year-old man, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch,
> showing old notes and photographs on a wooden table, Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> holding one photo with a trembling hand, warm lamp light, camera close-up

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Sudah berbulan-bulan aku menyelidiki vila ini. Kamu bukan tamu pertama."

## Scene 5 — Ryan Mengintip (20–25 detik)

**Visual:** Di balik pintu perpustakaan yang sedikit terbuka, Ryan mengintip dengan wajah marah, jam tangannya berkilau.

**Prompt:**
> Ryan, 32-year-old man, Olivia's husband, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch,
> peeking angrily through a slightly open library door at night, his expensive watch glinting,
> dark corridor behind him, camera low angle, slow push-in, tense, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Jadi ini rahasia yang kalian sembunyikan dariku."

## Scene 6 — Ending / Cliffhanger (25–30 detik)

**Visual:** Close-up mata Olivia, lalu layar menghitam. Teks: "Bersambung..."

**Prompt:**
> Close-up of Olivia's eyes, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> staring sharply at the camera, dark background, slowly fading to black,
> dramatic, cinematic, sharp eye detail

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Kalau kamu pewaris, kenapa Mia tidak pernah tahu?"
