# Episode 43 — Finale: Awal yang Baru (6 Scene × 5 detik)

Format: Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B, image-to-video, 480p, 24 fps.
Total: ~30 detik. Cocok untuk Shorts / Reels / TikTok vertikal (720x1280 di render,
atau render landscape lalu crop vertikal di CapCut).

## Kunci karakter (COPY-PASTE PERSIS di semua scene)

> **OLIVIA, wanita 28 tahun, rambut coklat panjang, gaun merah marun, kalung emas tipis.**
> **RYAN, pria 32 tahun, suami Olivia, jas navy, rambut hitam rapi, jam tangan mahal.**
> **JAKE, pria 33 tahun, suami Mia, kemeja putih lengan digulung, rambut coklat gelap, tatapan tenang, jam kulit hitam.**
> **MIA, wanita 29 tahun, sahabat Olivia, istri Jake, rambut pirang pendek, gaun hitam.**

Foto referensi: buat 1 foto tiap karakter dulu (Dreamina / Gemini image),
upload foto itu sebagai gambar awal di tiap scene (image-to-video).
Jangan ganti deskripsi di tengah episode.

## Setting global

- Negative prompt (tempel di semua scene):
  `blurry, low quality, distorted face, extra fingers, deformed hands, watermark, text, logo`
- Kamera: slow push-in / close-up, jangan pakai gerakan kamera ekstrem di T4 480p.
- Dialog: Wan 2.2 5B tidak bisa diandalkan untuk dialog Indonesia yang jelas.
  Render **tanpa suara dulu**, dubbing belakangan (CapCut TTS / ElevenLabs free),
  lalu tempel subtitle bakar di edit.

## Scene 1 — Olivia dan Jake di Jalan Danau (0–5 detik)

**Visual:** Pagi hari, Olivia dan Jake berjalan beriringan di jalan kecil tepi danau, membawa tas kecil, meninggalkan vila di belakang mereka.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, woman 28 years old, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> and Jake, man 33 years old, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch,
> walking side by side on a small lakeside road in the morning, carrying small bags,
> leaving a villa behind them, hopeful faces, soft sunlight, camera medium shot slowly moving closer, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Tidak ada lagi retreat, tidak ada lagi permainan. Mulai hari ini, hidup kita milik kita."

## Scene 2 — Mia Berdamai (5–10 detik)

**Visual:** Mia menghampiri mereka di jalan, tersenyum, lalu memeluk Olivia. Jake tersenyum lega melihat mereka berdamai.

**Prompt:**
> Mia, woman 29 years old, short blonde hair, black dress,
> hugging Olivia, woman 28 years old, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> on a lakeside road in the morning, Jake, man 33 years old, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves,
> watching them with a relieved smile, soft sunlight, camera medium shot, warm and emotional

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Kita sudah melewati semuanya. Persahabatan kita... tetap utuh."

## Scene 3 — Ryan Menebus Kesalahan (10–15 detik)

**Visual:** Ryan berdiri di depan vila yang hangus, membantu ayah Jake berjalan menuju mobil, lalu menoleh ke arah Olivia dan melambaikan tangan.

**Prompt:**
> Ryan, man 32 years old, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch,
> helping an elderly man in an elegant suit walk toward a car in front of a burnt lakeside villa in the morning,
> then turning and waving toward Olivia in the distance, calm and redeemed face,
> soft sunlight, camera medium shot, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Aku akan memperbaiki semuanya dari sini. Semoga kalian bahagia."

## Scene 4 — Ayah Jake Mengangguk (15–20 detik)

**Visual:** Ayah Jake duduk di dalam mobil, menatap Jake dari jendela, mengangguk perlahan sebagai restu.

**Prompt:**
> An elderly man in an elegant suit sitting inside a car in front of a burnt lakeside villa in the morning,
> looking out the window at Jake, man 33 years old, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze,
> nodding slowly in blessing, Jake nodding back, soft sunlight, camera close-up, emotional

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Pergilah, Nak. Kali ini, ayah tidak akan menghalangimu lagi."

## Scene 5 — Olivia dan Jake Bergandengan (20–25 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia dan Jake bergandengan tangan, berjalan menjauh di jalan tepi danau, Mia melambaikan tangan dari belakang.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, woman 28 years old, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> and Jake, man 33 years old, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch,
> holding hands and walking away on a lakeside road in the morning,
> Mia, woman 29 years old, short blonde hair, black dress, waving from behind them,
> soft sunlight, camera wide shot slowly moving closer, hopeful and warm, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Kita mulai dari nol. Bersama."

## Scene 6 — Penutup / Finale (25–30 detik)

**Visual:** Vila danau dari jauh saat matahari terbit, cahaya keemasan di atas air. Teks: "TAMAT — Love Is A Dangerous Game."

**Prompt:**
> Wide shot of a lakeside villa from far away at sunrise, golden light over calm lake water,
> birds in the sky, peaceful morning, warm golden tones,
> camera slow pull-back, cinematic, hopeful ending

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Cinta yang berbahaya... akhirnya menemukan jalannya pulang. Tamat."
