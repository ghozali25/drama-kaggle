# Episode 29 — Serangan di Hutan (6 Scene × 5 detik)

Format: Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B, image-to-video, 480p, 24 fps.
Total: ~30 detik. Cocok untuk Shorts / Reels / TikTok vertikal (720x1280 di render,
atau render landscape lalu crop vertikal di CapCut).

## Kunci karakter (COPY-PASTE PERSIS di semua scene)

> **OLIVIA, wanita 28 tahun, rambut coklat panjang, gaun merah marun, kalung emas tipis.**
> **RYAN, pria 32 tahun, suami Olivia, jas navy, rambut hitam rapi, jam tangan mahal.**
> **JAKE, pria 33 tahun, suami Mia, kemeja putih lengan digulung, rambut coklat gelap, tatapan tenang, jam kulit hitam.**
> **MIA, wanita 29 tahun, sahabat Olivia, istri Jake, rambut pirang pendek, gaun hitam.**

Foto referensi: buat 1 foto Olivia + 1 foto Ryan + 1 foto Jake + 1 foto Mia dulu (Dreamina / Gemini image),
upload foto itu sebagai gambar awal di tiap scene (image-to-video).
Jangan ganti deskripsi di tengah episode.

## Setting global

- Negative prompt (tempel di semua scene):
  `blurry, low quality, distorted face, extra fingers, deformed hands, watermark, text, logo`
- Kamera: slow push-in / close-up, jangan pakai gerakan kamera ekstrem di T4 480p.
- Dialog: Wan 2.2 5B tidak bisa diandalkan untuk dialog Indonesia yang jelas.
  Render **tanpa suara dulu**, dubbing belakangan (CapCut TTS / ElevenLabs free),
  lalu tempel subtitle bakar di edit.

## Scene 1 — Hook (0–5 detik)

**Visual:** Sore hari di jalan setapak hutan dekat danau. Jake berjalan sendiri, daun kering berderak di bawah kakinya.

**Prompt:**
> Jake, 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch,
> walking alone on a forest path near a lake in the late afternoon, dry leaves crunching under his feet,
> tall trees around him, camera slow push-in, cinematic, tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Aku tahu aku diikuti. Keluarlah."

## Scene 2 — Penyergapan (5–10 detik)

**Visual:** Dua pria bertubuh besar keluar dari balik pohon dan mengepung Jake. Jake berdiri tenang, mengukur mereka.

**Prompt:**
> Jake, 33-year-old man, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch,
> surrounded by two large men stepping out from behind trees on a forest path,
> standing calmly and sizing them up, late afternoon light, camera medium shot, tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Pemilik vila yang menyuruh kalian, kan?"

## Scene 3 — Perkelahian (10–15 detik)

**Visual:** Jake menangkis pukulan salah satu penyerang, gerakannya cepat dan terlatih. Daun-daun beterbangan.

**Prompt:**
> Jake, 33-year-old man, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze,
> blocking a punch from a large attacker on a forest path, fast trained movement, leaves flying,
> late afternoon, camera close-up, slow motion, tense, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Kalian salah orang kalau mengira aku tidak bisa melawan."

## Scene 4 — Olivia Datang (15–20 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia berlari masuk ke hutan sambil membawa ranting kayu besar, wajahnya panik tapi berani.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> running into the forest carrying a large wooden branch, her face panicked but brave,
> late afternoon light through the trees, camera medium shot, tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Jake! Aku di sini! Jangan sentuh dia!"

## Scene 5 — Olivia Menolong Jake (20–25 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia memukulkan ranting itu ke arah penyerang kedua, membuat penyerang itu mundur. Jake memanfaatkan celah itu untuk berdiri tegak.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> swinging a wooden branch at a second attacker, forcing him back, Jake, 33-year-old man, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves,
> using the opening to stand up straight, forest path, camera close-up, dramatic, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Aku tidak akan membiarkan mereka membawamu."

## Scene 6 — Ending / Cliffhanger (25–30 detik)

**Visual:** Close-up mata Olivia yang berani, lalu layar menghitam. Teks: "Bersambung..."

**Prompt:**
> Close-up of Olivia's brave eyes, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> staring at the camera, dark background, slowly fading to black,
> dramatic, cinematic, sharp eye detail

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Mereka lari, tapi Jake terluka. Dan lukanya tidak ringan."
