# Episode 21 — Olivia Melarikan Diri (6 Scene × 5 detik)

Format: Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B, image-to-video, 480p, 24 fps.
Total: ~30 detik. Cocok untuk Shorts / Reels / TikTok vertikal (720x1280 di render,
atau render landscape lalu crop vertikal di CapCut).

## Kunci karakter (COPY-PASTE PERSIS di semua scene)

> **OLIVIA, wanita 28 tahun, rambut coklat panjang, gaun merah marun, kalung emas tipis.**
> **RYAN, pria 32 tahun, suami Olivia, jas navy, rambut hitam rapi, jam tangan mahal.**
> **JAKE, pria 33 tahun, suami Mia, kemeja putih lengan digulung, rambut coklat gelap, tatapan tenang, jam kulit hitam.**
> **MIA, wanita 29 tahun, sahabat Olivia, istri Jake, rambut pirang pendek, gaun hitam.**

Foto referensi: buat 1 foto Olivia + 1 foto Ryan + 1 foto Jake + 1 foto Mia dulu
(Dreamina / Gemini image), upload foto itu sebagai gambar awal di tiap scene
(image-to-video). Jangan ganti deskripsi di tengah episode.

## Setting global

- Negative prompt (tempel di semua scene):
  `blurry, low quality, distorted face, extra fingers, deformed hands, watermark, text, logo`
- Kamera: slow push-in / close-up, jangan pakai gerakan kamera ekstrem di T4 480p.
- Dialog: Wan 2.2 5B tidak bisa diandalkan untuk dialog Indonesia yang jelas.
  Render **tanpa suara dulu**, dubbing belakangan (CapCut TTS / ElevenLabs free),
  lalu tempel subtitle bakar di edit.
- Lokasi: vila tepi danau dan hutan di sekitarnya, tengah malam, kabut, bulan purnama.

## Scene 1 — Hook (0–5 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia berlari keluar dari pintu vila, gaun merah marunnya berkibar, rambutnya terurai oleh angin malam.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, a 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> running out of a lakeside villa door at midnight, maroon dress flowing, long brown hair blown by night wind,
> moonlight and fog, tense, wide shot, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Aku tidak akan jadi jaminan siapa pun. Aku pergi."

## Scene 2 — Ryan Mengejar (5–10 detik)

**Visual:** Ryan berlari keluar menyusul, jas navynya terbuka, berteriak memanggil nama Olivia.

**Prompt:**
> Ryan, a 32-year-old man, Olivia's husband, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch,
> running out of a lakeside villa at midnight after Olivia, a 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> navy suit open, calling her name, moonlight and fog, tense, medium shot, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Olivia! Berhenti! Hutan itu berbahaya!"

## Scene 3 — Jake Mengejar (10–15 detik)

**Visual:** Jake keluar dari sisi lain vila, berlari ke arah hutan dengan langkah tenang tapi cepat, matanya mencari Olivia.

**Prompt:**
> Jake, a 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch,
> running from the side of a lakeside villa toward the forest at midnight, calm but fast, searching for Olivia,
> moonlight and fog, tense, medium shot, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Olivia! Ikuti suaraku — jangan lari lebih dalam!"

## Scene 4 — Olivia di Hutan (15–20 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia berlari di antara pepohonan, tersandung akar, lalu bersembunyi di balik pohon besar, napasnya berat.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, a 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> running between trees in a foggy forest at midnight, tripping on a root, then hiding behind a large tree, breathing heavily,
> moonlight through branches, close-up, tense, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Aku tidak tahu lagi siapa yang bisa kupercaya."

## Scene 5 — Dua Suara (20–25 detik)

**Visual:** Dari dua arah berbeda, suara Ryan dan Jake memanggil namanya. Olivia menutup mulut, tidak tahu harus menjawab siapa.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, a 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> hiding behind a large tree in a foggy forest at midnight, covering her mouth, hearing two voices calling her name from different directions,
> Ryan, a 32-year-old man, Olivia's husband, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch, and Jake, a 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch, approaching through the fog,
> tense, close-up, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Dua suara memanggilku. Satu suamiku... satu orang yang menyelamatkanku."

## Scene 6 — Ending / Cliffhanger (25–30 detik)

**Visual:** Cahaya senter menyapu pepohonan dan berhenti tepat di pohon tempat Olivia bersembunyi, lalu layar menghitam. Teks: "Bersambung..."

**Prompt:**
> A flashlight beam sweeping through foggy forest trees at midnight, stopping on the tree where Olivia, a 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace, is hiding,
> then slowly fading to black, dramatic, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Episode 22 — di tepi jurang, Jake membuka identitas aslinya."
