# Episode 27 — Gelas yang Salah (6 Scene × 5 detik)

Format: Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B, image-to-video, 480p, 24 fps.
Total: ~30 detik. Cocok untuk Shorts / Reels / TikTok vertikal (720x1280 di render,
atau render landscape lalu crop vertikal di CapCut).

## Kunci karakter (COPY-PASTE PERSIS di semua scene)

> **OLIVIA, wanita 28 tahun, rambut coklat panjang, gaun merah marun, kalung emas tipis.**
> **RYAN, pria 32 tahun, suami Olivia, jas navy, rambut hitam rapi, jam tangan mahal.**
> **JAKE, pria 33 tahun, suami Mia, kemeja putih lengan digulung, rambut coklat gelap, tatapan tenang, jam kulit hitam.**
> **MIA, wanita 29 tahun, sahabat Olivia, istri Jake, rambut pirang pendek, gaun hitam.**

Foto referensi: buat 1 foto Olivia + 1 foto Ryan + 1 foto Jake + 1 foto Mia dulu (Dreamina / Gemini image),
upload foto itu sebagai gambar awal di tiap scene (image-to-video).
Jangan ganti deskripsi di tengah episode.

## Setting global

- Negative prompt (tempel di semua scene):
  `blurry, low quality, distorted face, extra fingers, deformed hands, watermark, text, logo`
- Kamera: slow push-in / close-up, jangan pakai gerakan kamera ekstrem di T4 480p.
- Dialog: Wan 2.2 5B tidak bisa diandalkan untuk dialog Indonesia yang jelas.
  Render **tanpa suara dulu**, dubbing belakangan (CapCut TTS / ElevenLabs free),
  lalu tempel subtitle bakar di edit.

## Scene 1 — Hook (0–5 detik)

**Visual:** Malam hari di ruang makan vila. Ryan mengangkat gelas anggur ke bibirnya, lilin bergetar di atas meja.

**Prompt:**
> Ryan, 32-year-old man, Olivia's husband, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch,
> raising a wine glass to his lips at a candlelit dining table in a villa at night,
> candle flames flickering, camera slow push-in, cinematic, tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Satu tegukan saja, lalu semua selesai."

## Scene 2 — Olivia Berteriak (5–10 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia berdiri tiba-tiba dari kursinya, tangannya terulur ke arah Ryan, wajahnya panik.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> standing up suddenly from a candlelit dining table, reaching her hand toward Ryan, 32-year-old man, navy suit,
> her face panicked, night, camera medium shot, tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Ryan, berhenti! Gelas itu bukan untukmu!"

## Scene 3 — Jake Bertindak (10–15 detik)

**Visual:** Jake bergerak cepat, tangannya menyambar gelas Ryan dan menukarnya dengan gelas lain di atas meja.

**Prompt:**
> Jake, 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch,
> moving fast to grab Ryan's wine glass and swap it with another glass on a candlelit dining table,
> night, camera close-up, slow motion, tense, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Maaf, Ryan. Gelasmu tertukar denganku."

## Scene 4 — Keheningan (15–20 detik)

**Visual:** Semua orang di meja makan membeku. Pemilik vila menatap Jake dengan dingin, senyumnya hilang.

**Prompt:**
> An old villa owner staring coldly at Jake, 33-year-old man, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze,
> everyone frozen at a candlelit dining table, Ryan, 32-year-old man, navy suit, and Mia, 29-year-old woman, short blonde hair, black dress,
> holding their breath, night, camera slow push-in, tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Kamu tahu terlalu banyak, Jake."

## Scene 5 — Gelas Beracun Terungkap (20–25 detik)

**Visual:** Jake meletakkan gelas yang ditukar itu di atas piring putih. Cairannya meninggalkan noda gelap di piring.

**Prompt:**
> Jake, 33-year-old man, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch,
> placing the swapped wine glass on a white plate, its liquid leaving a dark stain,
> candlelit dining table at night, camera close-up, dramatic, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Lihat. Inilah yang seharusnya diminum Ryan malam ini."

## Scene 6 — Ending / Cliffhanger (25–30 detik)

**Visual:** Close-up mata Jake yang tenang menatap pemilik vila, lalu layar menghitam. Teks: "Bersambung..."

**Prompt:**
> Close-up of Jake's calm eyes, 33-year-old man, dark brown hair, calm gaze, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves,
> staring at the camera, dark background, slowly fading to black,
> dramatic, cinematic, sharp eye detail

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Racun itu bukan untuk Ryan. Racun itu untukku."
