# Episode 11 — Surat Tanpa Nama (6 Scene × 5 detik)

Format: Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B, image-to-video, 480p, 24 fps.
Total: ~30 detik. Cocok untuk Shorts / Reels / TikTok vertikal (720x1280 di render,
atau render landscape lalu crop vertikal di CapCut).

## Kunci karakter (COPY-PASTE PERSIS di semua scene)

> **OLIVIA, wanita 28 tahun, rambut coklat panjang, gaun merah marun, kalung emas tipis.**
> **RYAN, pria 32 tahun, suami Olivia, jas navy, rambut hitam rapi, jam tangan mahal.**
> **JAKE, pria 33 tahun, suami Mia, kemeja putih lengan digulung, rambut coklat gelap, tatapan tenang, jam kulit hitam.**
> **MIA, wanita 29 tahun, sahabat Olivia, istri Jake, rambut pirang pendek, gaun hitam.**

Foto referensi: buat 1 foto Olivia + 1 foto Ryan + 1 foto Jake + 1 foto Mia dulu
(Dreamina / Gemini image), upload foto itu sebagai gambar awal di tiap scene
(image-to-video). Jangan ganti deskripsi di tengah episode.

## Setting global

- Negative prompt (tempel di semua scene):
  `blurry, low quality, distorted face, extra fingers, deformed hands, watermark, text, logo`
- Kamera: slow push-in / close-up, jangan pakai gerakan kamera ekstrem di T4 480p.
- Dialog: Wan 2.2 5B tidak bisa diandalkan untuk dialog Indonesia yang jelas.
  Render **tanpa suara dulu**, dubbing belakangan (CapCut TTS / ElevenLabs free),
  lalu tempel subtitle bakar di edit.

## Scene 1 — Hook (0–5 detik)

**Visual:** Pagi hari, Olivia kembali ke kamar dan menemukan sebuah amplop putih tanpa nama di atas bantalnya.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace,
> returning to her villa bedroom in the morning and finding a white envelope
> with no name on her pillow, morning light through the window,
> camera medium shot slowly pushing in, cinematic, suspenseful

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Amplop ini... siapa yang meletakkannya di sini?"

## Scene 2 — Membuka Surat (5–10 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia membuka amplop dengan tangan gemetar. Di dalamnya hanya satu baris tulisan tangan.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace,
> opening a white envelope with trembling hands in a villa bedroom in the morning,
> one line of handwriting inside, morning light, camera close-up on the letter, slow push-in

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Satu baris saja..."

## Scene 3 — Isi Surat (10–15 detik)

**Visual:** Close-up tulisan tangan di surat itu: "Jangan percaya suamimu." Wajah Olivia pucat.

**Prompt:**
> Close-up of a handwritten letter reading "Jangan percaya suamimu", Olivia,
> 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace, holding the letter,
> her face turning pale, villa bedroom in the morning, camera close-up, suspenseful

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Jangan percaya suamimu."

## Scene 4 — Siapa Pengirimnya (15–20 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia memeriksa amplop, tidak ada perangko, tidak ada nama. Ia menatap pintu kamar yang sedikit terbuka.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace,
> checking a white envelope, no stamp, no name, looking at her bedroom door
> slightly open, villa bedroom in the morning, camera medium shot, slow push-in, tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Tidak ada perangko. Surat ini dikirim dari dalam vila."

## Scene 5 — Jake di Lorong (20–25 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia keluar ke lorong dan melihat Jake berdiri di ujung lorong, menatapnya dengan tenang, seolah menunggu.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace,
> stepping into a villa corridor in the morning and seeing Jake, 33-year-old man,
> Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze,
> black leather watch, standing at the end of the corridor, looking at her calmly,
> as if waiting, camera medium shot, slow push-in

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Jake... apakah surat ini darimu?"

## Scene 6 — Ending / Cliffhanger (25–30 detik)

**Visual:** Jake tidak menjawab, hanya menatap surat di tangan Olivia, lalu menoleh ke arah kamar Ryan. Layar menghitam. Teks: "Bersambung..."

**Prompt:**
> Jake, 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled sleeves, dark brown hair,
> calm gaze, black leather watch, not answering, looking at the letter in Olivia's hand,
> 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace, then turning
> toward Ryan's bedroom door, villa corridor in the morning, slowly fading to black,
> dramatic, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Episode 12 — apa yang disembunyikan Ryan dariku?"
