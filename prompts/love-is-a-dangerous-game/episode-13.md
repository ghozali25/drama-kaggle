# Episode 13 — Utang Ryan (6 Scene × 5 detik)

Format: Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B, image-to-video, 480p, 24 fps.
Total: ~30 detik. Cocok untuk Shorts / Reels / TikTok vertikal (720x1280 di render,
atau render landscape lalu crop vertikal di CapCut).

## Kunci karakter (COPY-PASTE PERSIS di semua scene)

> **OLIVIA, wanita 28 tahun, rambut coklat panjang, gaun merah marun, kalung emas tipis.**
> **RYAN, pria 32 tahun, suami Olivia, jas navy, rambut hitam rapi, jam tangan mahal.**
> **JAKE, pria 33 tahun, suami Mia, kemeja putih lengan digulung, rambut coklat gelap, tatapan tenang, jam kulit hitam.**
> **MIA, wanita 29 tahun, sahabat Olivia, istri Jake, rambut pirang pendek, gaun hitam.**

Foto referensi: buat 1 foto Olivia + 1 foto Ryan + 1 foto Jake + 1 foto Mia dulu
(Dreamina / Gemini image), upload foto itu sebagai gambar awal di tiap scene
(image-to-video). Jangan ganti deskripsi di tengah episode.

## Setting global

- Negative prompt (tempel di semua scene):
  `blurry, low quality, distorted face, extra fingers, deformed hands, watermark, text, logo`
- Kamera: slow push-in / close-up, jangan pakai gerakan kamera ekstrem di T4 480p.
- Dialog: Wan 2.2 5B tidak bisa diandalkan untuk dialog Indonesia yang jelas.
  Render **tanpa suara dulu**, dubbing belakangan (CapCut TTS / ElevenLabs free),
  lalu tempel subtitle bakar di edit.
- Lokasi: vila mewah tepi danau, ruang kerja dan koridor vila, malam hari.

## Scene 1 — Hook (0–5 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia menemukan secarik kertas tagihan di saku jas Ryan yang tergantung di kursi.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, a 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> finding a paper bill inside the pocket of a navy suit jacket hanging on a chair,
> inside a luxurious lakeside villa at night, warm lamp light, shocked expression, close-up, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Tagihan... atas nama Ryan. Angkanya tidak masuk akal."

## Scene 2 — Angka Utang (5–10 detik)

**Visual:** Close-up kertas itu — angka besar dan cap pemilik vila. Tangan Olivia gemetar.

**Prompt:**
> Close-up of a paper bill with a large amount and a villa owner's stamp, held by Olivia, a 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> trembling hand, inside a luxurious lakeside villa at night, warm lamp light, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Ratusan juta. Dan nama pemilik vila ini ada di bawahnya."

## Scene 3 — Konfrontasi (10–15 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia menghadang Ryan di koridor, menunjukkan kertas itu. Ryan terkejut lalu menunduk.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, a 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> confronting Ryan, a 32-year-old man, Olivia's husband, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch, in a villa corridor at night,
> holding up the paper bill, Ryan shocked then looking down, tense, medium shot, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Jelaskan. Sejak kapan kamu berutang pada pemilik vila ini?"

## Scene 4 — Pengakuan Ryan (15–20 detik)

**Visual:** Ryan bersandar ke dinding, menutup wajah dengan tangan, jam mahalnya berkilat.

**Prompt:**
> Ryan, a 32-year-old man, Olivia's husband, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch,
> leaning against a villa corridor wall at night, covering his face with his hand, expensive watch glinting,
> Olivia, a 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace, watching him, close-up, tense, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Bisnisku bangkrut setahun lalu. Aku pinjam uang... dan vila ini bunganya."

## Scene 5 — Jake Mendengar (20–25 detik)

**Visual:** Di ujung koridor, Jake berdiri diam mendengarkan, tatapan tenangnya berubah tajam.

**Prompt:**
> Jake, a 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch,
> standing silently at the end of a villa corridor at night, listening, calm gaze turning sharp,
> warm dim light, slow push-in, tense, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Jadi itu caranya. Ryan tidak membawa Olivia ke sini untuk liburan."

## Scene 6 — Ending / Cliffhanger (25–30 detik)

**Visual:** Close-up wajah Olivia yang kecewa, lalu layar menghitam. Teks: "Bersambung..."

**Prompt:**
> Close-up of Olivia's face, a 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> disappointed and hurt expression, dark villa corridor background, then slowly fading to black,
> dramatic, cinematic, sharp eye detail

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Episode 14 — ada apa di sayap vila yang terkunci?"
