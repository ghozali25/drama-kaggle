# Episode 41 — Kebakaran Vila (6 Scene × 5 detik)

Format: Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B, image-to-video, 480p, 24 fps.
Total: ~30 detik. Cocok untuk Shorts / Reels / TikTok vertikal (720x1280 di render,
atau render landscape lalu crop vertikal di CapCut).

## Kunci karakter (COPY-PASTE PERSIS di semua scene)

> **OLIVIA, wanita 28 tahun, rambut coklat panjang, gaun merah marun, kalung emas tipis.**
> **RYAN, pria 32 tahun, suami Olivia, jas navy, rambut hitam rapi, jam tangan mahal.**
> **JAKE, pria 33 tahun, suami Mia, kemeja putih lengan digulung, rambut coklat gelap, tatapan tenang, jam kulit hitam.**
> **MIA, wanita 29 tahun, sahabat Olivia, istri Jake, rambut pirang pendek, gaun hitam.**

Foto referensi: buat 1 foto tiap karakter dulu (Dreamina / Gemini image),
upload foto itu sebagai gambar awal di tiap scene (image-to-video).
Jangan ganti deskripsi di tengah episode.

## Setting global

- Negative prompt (tempel di semua scene):
  `blurry, low quality, distorted face, extra fingers, deformed hands, watermark, text, logo`
- Kamera: slow push-in / close-up, jangan pakai gerakan kamera ekstrem di T4 480p.
- Dialog: Wan 2.2 5B tidak bisa diandalkan untuk dialog Indonesia yang jelas.
  Render **tanpa suara dulu**, dubbing belakangan (CapCut TTS / ElevenLabs free),
  lalu tempel subtitle bakar di edit.

## Scene 1 — Asap dari Ruang Studi (0–5 detik)

**Visual:** Malam hari, asap mulai mengepul dari bawah pintu ruang studi. Olivia mencium bau terbakar dan menoleh panik.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, woman 28 years old, long brown hair, maroon red dress, thin gold necklace,
> smelling smoke and turning in panic as smoke rises under a study door in a villa at night,
> orange glow growing behind the door, frightened face, camera slow push-in, tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Api! Ada yang membakar ruang studi!"

## Scene 2 — Ayah Jake Menolak Pergi (5–10 detik)

**Visual:** Ayah Jake berdiri di tengah ruang studi yang berasap, memandangi lukisan keluarga di dinding, enggan pergi.

**Prompt:**
> An elderly man in an elegant suit standing in a smoke-filled villa study at night,
> looking at a large family painting on the wall, refusing to leave,
> orange firelight flickering, Jake pulling at his arm, camera medium shot, dramatic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Ayah, tinggalkan lukisan itu! Nyawamu lebih penting!"

## Scene 3 — Jake Menyeret Ayahnya Keluar (10–15 detik)

**Visual:** Jake merangkul ayahnya dan menyeretnya keluar dari ruang studi yang terbakar, Olivia membuka pintu untuk mereka.

**Prompt:**
> Jake, man 33 years old, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves, dark brown hair, calm gaze, black leather watch,
> dragging his elderly father out of a burning villa study at night,
> Olivia, woman 28 years old, long brown hair, maroon red dress, holding the door open,
> smoke and orange firelight, camera low angle, urgent movement, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Pegangan padaku, Ayah. Kita keluar bersama."

## Scene 4 — Ryan dan Mia di Tangga (15–20 detik)

**Visual:** Ryan dan Mia berlari menuruni tangga utama sambil berteriak memperingatkan tamu pesta topeng yang masih di aula.

**Prompt:**
> Ryan, man 32 years old, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch,
> and Mia, woman 29 years old, short blonde hair, black dress,
> running down a grand villa staircase at night, shouting warnings to masked guests in the hall below,
> smoke rising, chandelier light flickering, camera wide shot, urgent

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Semua keluar! Vila terbakar, jangan panik, ikuti kami!"

## Scene 5 — Semua Berlari Keluar (20–25 detik)

**Visual:** Semua orang berlari keluar dari pintu utama vila ke halaman, api membesar di jendela belakang mereka.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, woman 28 years old, long brown hair, maroon red dress,
> Jake, man 33 years old, white shirt with rolled-up sleeves,
> Ryan, man 32 years old, navy suit, and Mia, woman 29 years old, short blonde hair, black dress,
> running out of the main door of a lakeside villa at night toward the garden,
> fire growing in the windows behind them, orange light on their faces,
> camera wide shot, dramatic, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Terus lari! Jangan berhenti sampai di tepi danau!"

## Scene 6 — Ending / Cliffhanger (25–30 detik)

**Visual:** Vila terbakar di kejauhan, keempatnya berdiri di halaman menatap api, lalu layar menghitam. Teks: "Bersambung..."

**Prompt:**
> Wide shot of a lakeside villa burning at night, flames in the windows,
> Olivia, Jake, Ryan, and Mia standing in the garden watching the fire,
> orange light on their faces, lake behind them, camera slowly pulling back,
> slowly fading to black, dramatic, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Episode 42 — di tepi danau, Ryan akhirnya melepaskan Olivia."
