# Episode 10 — Hujan dan Danau (6 Scene × 5 detik)

Format: Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B, image-to-video, 480p, 24 fps.
Total: ~30 detik. Cocok untuk Shorts / Reels / TikTok vertikal (720x1280 di render,
atau render landscape lalu crop vertikal di CapCut).

## Kunci karakter (COPY-PASTE PERSIS di semua scene)

> **OLIVIA, wanita 28 tahun, rambut coklat panjang, gaun merah marun, kalung emas tipis.**
> **RYAN, pria 32 tahun, suami Olivia, jas navy, rambut hitam rapi, jam tangan mahal.**
> **JAKE, pria 33 tahun, suami Mia, kemeja putih lengan digulung, rambut coklat gelap, tatapan tenang, jam kulit hitam.**
> **MIA, wanita 29 tahun, sahabat Olivia, istri Jake, rambut pirang pendek, gaun hitam.**

Foto referensi: buat 1 foto Olivia + 1 foto Ryan + 1 foto Jake + 1 foto Mia dulu
(Dreamina / Gemini image), upload foto itu sebagai gambar awal di tiap scene
(image-to-video). Jangan ganti deskripsi di tengah episode.

## Setting global

- Negative prompt (tempel di semua scene):
  `blurry, low quality, distorted face, extra fingers, deformed hands, watermark, text, logo`
- Kamera: slow push-in / close-up, jangan pakai gerakan kamera ekstrem di T4 480p.
- Dialog: Wan 2.2 5B tidak bisa diandalkan untuk dialog Indonesia yang jelas.
  Render **tanpa suara dulu**, dubbing belakangan (CapCut TTS / ElevenLabs free),
  lalu tempel subtitle bakar di edit.

## Scene 1 — Hook (0–5 detik)

**Visual:** Sore hari, langit mendung di atas danau. Olivia berdiri sendirian di dermaga, menatap air yang gelap.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace,
> standing alone on a lake pier in the afternoon, dark clouds above, looking at the dark water,
> wind blowing her hair, camera wide shot slowly pushing in, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Semua orang di vila ini menyembunyikan sesuatu dariku."

## Scene 2 — Hujan Turun (5–10 detik)

**Visual:** Hujan turun tiba-tiba. Papan dermaga licin. Olivia melangkah mundur, sepatunya tergelincir.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace,
> stepping back on a slippery lake pier as rain suddenly falls, her shoe slipping,
> dark lake below, rain, camera medium shot, slow movement, tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Hujannya deras sekali..."

## Scene 3 — Hampir Jatuh (10–15 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia kehilangan keseimbangan di tepi dermaga, tangannya meraih udara kosong. Air danau gelap di bawahnya.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace,
> losing her balance at the edge of a lake pier in the rain, reaching out at empty air,
> dark lake water below, camera low angle, slow movement, tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Tolong!"

## Scene 4 — Jake Menyelamatkan (15–20 detik)

**Visual:** Jake berlari dari arah vila dan menarik Olivia dari tepi dermaga. Keduanya basah kuyup, saling menatap.

**Prompt:**
> Jake, 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled sleeves, dark brown hair,
> calm gaze, black leather watch, running from the villa and pulling Olivia,
> 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace, away from the edge
> of a lake pier in the rain, both soaking wet, looking at each other,
> camera medium shot, slow push-in, dramatic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Aku di sini. Kamu aman sekarang."

## Scene 5 — Ryan Melihat (20–25 detik)

**Visual:** Ryan berdiri di pintu vila, menyaksikan Jake memegang Olivia. Wajahnya merah menahan marah, tapi ia tidak bergerak.

**Prompt:**
> Ryan, 32-year-old man, Olivia's husband, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch,
> standing at the villa door in the rain, watching Jake holding Olivia on the lake pier,
> face flushed with anger, not moving, camera medium shot, tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Lepaskan istriku."

## Scene 6 — Ending / Cliffhanger (25–30 detik)

**Visual:** Close-up kalung emas Olivia yang putus dan jatuh ke papan dermaga — Jake memungutnya, lalu menatap ke arah vila. Layar menghitam. Teks: "Bersambung..."

**Prompt:**
> Close-up of Olivia's broken thin gold necklace falling onto the wet pier planks in the rain,
> Jake, 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled sleeves, dark brown hair,
> calm gaze, black leather watch, picking it up and looking toward the villa,
> rain, slowly fading to black, dramatic, cinematic, sharp detail

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Episode 11 — kalung ini kenapa terasa penting baginya?"
