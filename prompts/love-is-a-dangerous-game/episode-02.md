# Episode 02 — Kedatangan (6 Scene × 5 detik)

Format: Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B, image-to-video, 480p, 24 fps.
Total: ~30 detik. Cocok untuk Shorts / Reels / TikTok vertikal (720x1280 di render,
atau render landscape lalu crop vertikal di CapCut).

## Kunci karakter (COPY-PASTE PERSIS di semua scene)

> **OLIVIA, wanita 28 tahun, rambut coklat panjang, gaun merah marun, kalung emas tipis.**
> **RYAN, pria 32 tahun, suami Olivia, jas navy, rambut hitam rapi, jam tangan mahal.**
> **JAKE, pria 33 tahun, suami Mia, kemeja putih lengan digulung, rambut coklat gelap, tatapan tenang, jam kulit hitam.**
> **MIA, wanita 29 tahun, sahabat Olivia, istri Jake, rambut pirang pendek, gaun hitam.**

Foto referensi: buat 1 foto Olivia + 1 foto Ryan + 1 foto Jake + 1 foto Mia dulu
(Dreamina / Gemini image), upload foto itu sebagai gambar awal di tiap scene
(image-to-video). Jangan ganti deskripsi di tengah episode.

## Setting global

- Negative prompt (tempel di semua scene):
  `blurry, low quality, distorted face, extra fingers, deformed hands, watermark, text, logo`
- Kamera: slow push-in / close-up, jangan pakai gerakan kamera ekstrem di T4 480p.
- Dialog: Wan 2.2 5B tidak bisa diandalkan untuk dialog Indonesia yang jelas.
  Render **tanpa suara dulu**, dubbing belakangan (CapCut TTS / ElevenLabs free),
  lalu tempel subtitle bakar di edit.

## Scene 1 — Hook (0–5 detik)

**Visual:** Siang hari, mobil berhenti di depan vila mewah tepi danau. Olivia turun, menatap vila dengan curiga.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace,
> stepping out of a car in front of a luxurious lakeside villa in daylight,
> looking at the villa suspiciously, calm lake behind, camera wide shot slowly pushing in, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Vila ini... terlalu sepi untuk sebuah retreat."

## Scene 2 — Sambutan (5–10 detik)

**Visual:** Di pintu vila, seorang tuan rumah berjas abu-abu menyambut Ryan dan Olivia dengan senyum yang terlalu tenang.

**Prompt:**
> Ryan, 32-year-old man, Olivia's husband, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch,
> shaking hands with a mysterious villa host in a grey suit at the villa entrance,
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace,
> watching them warily, grand wooden door behind, camera medium shot static

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Selamat datang. Di sini, semua pasangan akan saling mengenal... lebih dalam."

## Scene 3 — Jake dan Mia (10–15 detik)

**Visual:** Jake dan Mia sudah tiba di teras. Jake menatap Olivia sebentar, tenang, lalu mengangguk sopan.

**Prompt:**
> Jake, 33-year-old man, Mia's husband, white shirt with rolled sleeves, dark brown hair,
> calm gaze, black leather watch, standing on a villa terrace, nodding politely at Olivia,
> 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace, Mia, 29-year-old woman,
> Olivia's best friend, Jake's wife, short blonde hair, black dress, smiling beside him,
> lake view behind, camera medium shot, slow push-in

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Akhirnya kamu datang juga, Liv."

## Scene 4 — Aturan Retreat (15–20 detik)

**Visual:** Di ruang utama, tuan rumah membacakan aturan dari kartu hitam: ponsel dikumpulkan, pasangan tidak boleh berpisah dari pasangannya.

**Prompt:**
> A mysterious villa host in a grey suit reading rules from a black card in a grand living room,
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace, and Ryan,
> 32-year-old man, Olivia's husband, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch, listening tensely,
> warm chandelier light, camera close-up on the black card, suspenseful

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Aturan pertama: selama retreat, tidak ada yang boleh keluar dari vila."

## Scene 5 — Ponsel Dikumpulkan (20–25 detik)

**Visual:** Olivia ragu-ragu menyerahkan ponselnya ke kotak kayu. Ryan langsung menyerahkan ponselnya tanpa ragu.

**Prompt:**
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace,
> hesitating as she places her phone into a wooden box, Ryan, 32-year-old man,
> Olivia's husband, navy suit, neat black hair, expensive watch, handing his phone over easily,
> other guests watching, camera close-up on the wooden box, tense

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Ryan, kenapa kamu tidak keberatan sama sekali?"

## Scene 6 — Ending / Cliffhanger (25–30 detik)

**Visual:** Pintu utama vila ditutup dan dikunci dari dalam. Olivia menoleh ke arah pintu, lalu layar menghitam. Teks: "Bersambung..."

**Prompt:**
> Close-up of a large villa door being closed and locked from the inside,
> Olivia, 28-year-old woman, long brown hair, maroon dress, thin gold necklace,
> turning to look at the door with a worried face, dark hallway, slowly fading to black,
> dramatic, cinematic

**Dialog (dubbing):** "Episode 3 — siapa yang menukar pasangan kita?"
